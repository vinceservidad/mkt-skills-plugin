# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Churn analysis

Measuring who leaves, when and why, by cohort and by type. Applied with denominators and windows, exit reasons counted and labelled self-reported, and each cause handed to its owner.

## Customer success

A proactive function that watches account health and helps customers reach their goals. Applied for B2B or high-value accounts with named risk signals and actions.

## Dunning / failed-payment recovery

Recovering subscriptions when a payment fails: notice, retries, an easy card update and a grace period. Applied first, because involuntary churn is customers who did not choose to leave.

## Exit surveys / save offers

Asking why at cancellation and responding to the reason with a pause, a change or a fitting option. Applied without obstruction; discounts only when price is the diagnosed reason and the owner sets them.
