# Meta Partnership Ads, Retailer Collaborative Ads and Product Seeding

Distilled from the Meta Blueprint Creative Strategy Professional study guide (July 2026) and creator seeding workflows. Keep contract execution, legal terms, and fee commitments with the owner; confirm Meta Partnership Ads permissions and Retailer Collaborative Ads availability in Business Manager before launch (*to confirm*).

## 1. Creator Partnership Ads vs. Retailer Collaborative Ads

Keep gifting, content commissioning, usage licensing, and ad-identity permission strictly separate. An organic post tagging the brand does **not** authorize paid amplification.

| Dimension | Meta Partnership Ads (Creator) | Collaborative Ads (Retailer Catalog) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary relationship | Brand + creator or partner | Brand + participating retail partner |
| Display identity and asset | Paid ad displays dual brand/creator identity or runs from creator post via partnership permission (`09-ugc-brief`) | Retailer shares a brand-specific catalog segment so ads drive directly to the retailer's product page/app |
| Destination and checkout | Brand's website, landing page, or app (`12-landing-page`) | Retailer's website, store, or app (brand's own checkout misses these orders) |
| Critical pre-launch gate | Explicit Partnership Ad code/permission, paid-usage term, approved claims, and FTC/platform disclosure (`20-compliance`) | Verified catalog segment mapping, stock parity, partner access, and agreed retailer sales reporting access |
| Owning handoff | `11-influencer` owns partner vetting and deal ledger; `09-ad-creative` owns concept; `11-paid-social` owns ad set/bidding | `11-marketplace-retail-media` owns retailer unit economics; `11-paid-social` owns campaign setup; `17-tracking-plan` owns retailer reporting access |

For every Partnership Ad, maintain a relationship ledger recording: partner handle, intended business outcome, approved assets, rights scope and expiry window, ad-code authorization state, verified destination URL, and disclosure status.

## 2. Product seeding pilot and authenticity screening

Product seeding sends product with **no guaranteed posting obligation** to observe genuine product fit before committing flat sponsorship or Partnership Ad budgets:
1. **Screen authenticity before shipping:** Inspect the creator's last 10 posts for ICP comment relevance (real buyer questions vs. engagement pods/spam), genuine category affinity, and competing brand conflicts (`scripts/sum_by.py` computes views-to-follower ratio cleanly). Never rely on raw follower count or unverified media-kit screenshots.
2. **Cost the entire pilot honestly:** Sum product COGS, custom packaging, shipping/fulfillment, and any outreach ops into real landed cost per gift sent. Never treat seeded gifts as "free media."
3. **Two-track outreach clarity:** Specify clearly in gifting outreach whether the invite is no-obligation product discovery (never silently convert a no-obligation gift into a demanded deliverable) vs. a paid sponsored brief (`09-ugc-brief`).
4. **Upgrade path:** Track dispatched -> delivered -> voluntary post -> permission requested -> unique-link orders/sign-ups. Only creators showing authentic product enthusiasm and ICP resonance move up to paid content licensing, affiliate links (`11-affiliate-jv`), or Meta Partnership Ads (`11-paid-social`).

## 3. Compensation menu (owner sets all rates)

- **Gifted / seeding:** Product cost only, zero guaranteed deliverables or ad rights.
- **Flat fee / UGC creation:** Paid per deliverable or raw footage bundle for brand-owned ad accounts (`09-ugc-brief`), keeping usage rights time-boxed (`3/6/12` months or perpetual owner-agreed scope).
- **Whitelisting / Partnership Ads fee:** Monthly handle-access fee plus agreed spend cap, stacked cleanly on top of production fees.
- **Hybrid base + affiliate commission:** Lower upfront fee paired with tracked first-order or retained-customer commission (`11-affiliate-jv`), aligned to unit-economics ceilings (`17-unit-economics`).
