# Objection guide

## Pattern

1. Acknowledge: show it was heard, without agreeing or arguing.
2. Ask: one question to find what is behind it (compared with what? what would need to be true?).
3. Answer: something true and specific from the offer, proof, guarantee or the buyer's own stated cost.
4. Check: does that answer it?

## Types

| Type | Sign | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Misfit | Outside the ICP, needs what the offer doesn't do | Say so, recommend what fits, exit politely |
| Missing information | Asks how something works | Answer plainly, show it |
| Belief | Thinks all options are the same, or it won't work for them | Proof, mechanism, story |
| Risk | Fears it fails | The guarantee as written, a smaller first step if one exists |
| Timing | Not now | Cost of waiting, a dated next step |
| Stall | Think about it | Ask what they'd think about; often another objection |
| Other decider | Partner, board | Meet them, equip the champion |

## Price

Value first (the cost of the problem in their numbers), then the alternatives they actually compare with. Discounts and payment plans only if the offer has them.

## Never

Argue, pressure after a no, make up proof, or offer a concession the owner hasn't set.
