# Experiment log guide

## Entry template

ID · Hypothesis · Change · Audience and page · Dates · Sample per arm · Primary metric (system of record) · Result (counts, interval) · Validity (valid / weak / invalid, with reason) · Insight (observed) · Mechanism guess (labelled) · Next step · Tags

## Validity

Weak or invalid if: stopped early on a tool's label, sample below plan, other changes during the test, metric from a defective source, unequal traffic split.

## Insight

Write what customers did, under which conditions. Keep the guessed reason separate.

## Weekly growth meeting

Review last week's results → update the log → pick next tests from the scored backlog → assign owners and dates.
