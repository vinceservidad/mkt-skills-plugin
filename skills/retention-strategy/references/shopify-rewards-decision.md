## Source and evidence boundary

[Shopify: How To Create a Rewards Program That Drives Revenue](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/create-rewards-program), published 2026-08-26; reviewed 2026-09-29.

Shopify organizes rewards design around goals, the behaviors to encourage, incentives and implementation capacity. It describes both financial and nonfinancial rewards. These are design options, not proof that a program increases this business's profit.

This is Shopify editorial guidance, not a controlled effectiveness study. The MKT application below is an original operational adaptation using this system's existing governance. No merchant result, benchmark, universal timing rule, or performance guarantee is imported. Verify current platform features separately before implementation.

## MKT application

First diagnose why eligible customers are not continuing. Rewards do not fix unresolved product failures, poor fit or broken fulfillment. If there is no meaningful repeat behavior, explain why a program is not the appropriate intervention instead of inventing one.

Specify a behavior that represents continuing value, the eligible cohort and observation window. Choose an incentive whose cost and customer value can be evaluated. Preserve consent and cancellation rights; do not reward only favorable reviews or manufacture social proof. Program terms, pricing and compliance remain decisions for their existing owners and qualified review where needed.

Ask `$retention-economics` to calculate the business-defined contribution basis and maturity limits. Include incentive, redemption, platform and servicing costs where relevant, distinguish earned obligations from redeemed cost, and never subtract a discount twice. Ask `$lifecycle-marketing` for communication mechanics and `$copywriting` for the requested messages.

Member-versus-nonmember performance is vulnerable to self-selection. Use `$tracking-measurement` to assess a comparison before claiming incremental retention. A synthetic equal-sized randomized comparison records 200 treated repeat orders and 180 control orders, with GBP 25 contribution per order before program costs: observed contribution difference is GBP 500. Incremental program costs of GBP 650 leave minus GBP 150 on that defined basis. More orders alone do not justify expansion; uncertainty, cost accounting and downstream behavior still matter.

**Deliverable:** diagnosed reason and eligibility, behavior/incentive choice, costed alternative, complete requested plan/messages, comparison design, guardrails and launch/hold/revise decision. Do not call a survey ROI claim, launch, enrollment count or short-term redemption a verified durable outcome.
