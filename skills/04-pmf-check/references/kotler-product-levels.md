# Five Product Levels and 4Cs (Kotler)

Use these two checks from Philip Kotler (*Marketing Management*) when auditing whether the product itself matches what the target segment actually buys. Keep all product facts grounded in `context/offer.md`, `context/customer.md`, and real customer evidence in `inputs/`.

## 1. Five Product Levels (Kotler)

Map every level from the business's real product and delivery records. Leave unverified levels blank or marked for confirmation.

| Level | Question | What to check in evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Core benefit | What outcome is the buyer actually paying for? | Main benefit cited by retained or very-disappointed users. |
| 2. Generic product | What basic version delivers that core benefit? | Core features or deliverables shipped today. |
| 3. Expected product | What baseline standards do buyers treat as table stakes? | Delivery speed, uptime, billing clarity, basic support tickets. |
| 4. Augmented product | What extras separate this product from baseline alternatives? | Onboarding help, templates, reporting, guarantees in `offer.md`. |
| 5. Potential product | Where could the product evolve next based on user demand? | Repeated feature or outcome requests from retained segments. |

Decision rule: fix missing Level 3 (Expected) basics before promoting Level 4 (Augmented) perks. If Expected quality breaks (defects, slow fulfilment, billing friction), more ad spend increases refunds and churn.

## 2. 4Ps to 4Cs buyer translation (Kotler)

Before tuning marketing around the seller's view (Product, Price, Place, Promotion), translate each element into the buyer's view:

- **Product -> Customer solution:** name the specific problem solved in that segment's own words, not internal feature names.
- **Price -> Customer cost:** account for money plus switching time, setup effort, learning curve, and risk. Direct pricing changes to `07-pricing`.
- **Place -> Convenience:** note how easily buyers find, evaluate, buy, and start using the product. Flag buying friction from support logs.
- **Promotion -> Communication:** replace one-way pitch claims with proof and dialogue that match buyer questions and reviews.
