## Verified source basis

[Google: ABCDs of effective video ads](https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/) (May 2023). The page attributes its reported research to Google/Kantar (April 2021); no underlying study was independently reviewed here.

Source review: 2026-09-29. The named source provides the framing below. The MKT application is an original operational adaptation, not a quotation, platform requirement or experimentally proven outcome. Account availability and current controls still require library/contracts/PLATFORM-CURRENCY.md; a review date does not make an old article current.

The source names Attention, Branding, Connection and Direction. Its recommendations cover an engaging opening, recognizable brand presence, a focused human/product story and a clear action. These source principles inform a creative review; the page's reported sales/brand-lift figures are not forecasts or guarantees for this user.

## MKT application

Use four explicit review rows and preserve their source names. Attention: what happens in the opening and why the evidenced audience would keep watching. Branding: how the product and brand are recognized. Connection: what supported experience or problem gives the story meaning. Direction: the exact requested next action and where it leads.

For each row, supply an observed timestamp or script line, the issue or strength, the proposed revision and the evidence needed. If only a script was supplied, label it a script review; do not claim to have inspected a rendered video, captions or audio. Source advice about brightness, pace and early branding is not a mandate to violate brand constraints or use one visual style everywhere.

When the user requests finished creative, coordinate `$creative-strategy` to return the actual concept, opening, shot sequence, overlay text, audio direction, proof and CTA, not just the letters ABCD. Keep authentic testimony and usage rights separate from a dramatized example. An unverified claim cannot be repaired by stronger storytelling.

The channel owner verifies the current placement, format, skip behavior and asset requirements before release. Fit the measurement to the objective: a completion rate diagnoses attention, not incremental sales. Use `$tracking-measurement` for attribution/lift interpretation and valid comparisons; do not call ABCD compliance a winning ad.

**Deliverable:** completed annotated review or coordinated production brief, substantiation gaps, current format checks, test variable, outcome/guardrails and draft/approved status. No media placement or campaign change is authorized by this method.
