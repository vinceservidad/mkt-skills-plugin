# Creative brief fields

| Field | Must contain | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | One action the work must cause, the channel, the traffic temperature | "Raise awareness and drive sales" (two jobs, no measure) |
| Audience | One segment from the ICP, in plain words | "Small businesses" (no segment) |
| Awareness level | One level from the awareness map, with its share as given | Mixing unaware and most-aware in one brief |
| Insight | A buyer truth with evidence (a VoC quote ID, a pain, a belief) | A product feature dressed as an insight |
| Single message | One sentence from the promise or one message-house pillar | Three messages "to cover everything" |
| Reason to believe | Two or three proof items, strongest first, with strength and permission | Invented stats; testimonials used without permission |
| Tone and brand | Voice rules and distinctive assets from `brand.md`, or "no brand voice on file" | Adjectives only ("bold, modern, fun") |
| Format and deliverables | Formats, sizes, lengths, number of concepts and variants; specs verified or *to confirm* | Specs from memory stated as current |
| Call to action and destination | One CTA to a real offer and page | A CTA to an offer that doesn't exist |
| Mandatories | Exact offer terms, required disclaimers, logo or asset use | Paraphrased terms that change the deal |
| Do not | Off-limits claims and why (unproven, contradicted, policy risk) | Missing, so the makers invent claims |
| How it will be judged | The business outcome that decides, and the approval step | "Engagement" or CTR alone |

## Concepts, not cosmetic variants

Ask the makers for distinct concepts (different ways into the single message) and, inside each, variants that change one element. Colour, crop and placement adaptations are not new concepts.

## One brief per level

If the campaign must reach two awareness levels, write two briefs. The insight, message and CTA differ by level.
