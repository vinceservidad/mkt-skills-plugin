# Research-to-Brief Ledger, Endorsements and Production Preflight

Use alongside `references/brief-fields.md` and `references/methods.md` when translating customer evidence, competitor ad references, or creator/customer quotes into an actionable creative brief.

## 1. Evidence-to-choice ledger

Record this chain for every major strategic decision in a brief so designers and editors know *why* a choice exists and what stays invariant:

| Field | What goes in the brief |
| --- | --- |
| Observation | Exact source location (`context/customer.md`, review ID, search report, ad library URL + date); separate internal results from market trends |
| Interpretation | Buyer friction or motive suggested + alternative explanation + confidence level |
| Hypothesis | What change should move which segment and measured business outcome |
| Creative choice | Single central message, mechanic, proof beat, visual direction, or format to test |
| Proof & constraints | Substantiated claims, `offer.md` mandatories, rights/permission gaps, production capacity limits |
| Test & next decision | Single variable tested, comparison baseline (`18-creative-testing`), business metric + stop condition |

Observed ad longevity, view count, or competitor spend hints at continuity, never conversion rate, profit, or causality.

## 2. Reverse-engineering supplied references cleanly

- Log observable structure from supplied competitor/mood references: attention device, reading order, product role, hierarchy, typography weight/contrast, whitespace, proof placement, CTA treatment, and brand codes.
- Translate the **underlying communication job** into original execution from this brand's own truth and `context/brand.md` codes. **Never copy distinctive trade dress, proprietary layouts, trademarked slogans, or borrow another company's customer reviews.**
- Replace generic aesthetic adjectives (*premium*, *modern*, *clean*) with concrete direction tied to `brand.md`, or mark styling *unverified against brand guide* under Open questions.

## 3. Endorsement & rights permission ledger

For any customer quote, creator clip, or third-party statement included in a brief:
- Record exact verbatim source (`reviews.csv` row / interview ID), speaker role/context, product match, capture date, **explicit ad-reuse permission status** (`granted` vs `missing — obtain before ad launch`), incentive/gift disclosure status (`yes/no`), and allowed claim scope.
- Never extrapolate a subjective quote on one SKU or feature into a general efficacy promise or apply one SKU's claim across an entire catalog bundle. Hand creator contracts/usage rights planning to `11-influencer` and regulated disclosures to `20-compliance`.

## 4. Production preflight before handoff

Verify before marking a brief ready for review:
1. One segment, one awareness level, one central proposition, one CTA matching an existing destination in `offer.md`.
2. Commercial terms (price, cadence, guarantee, shipping, expiry) match `offer.md` verbatim — stop and confirm missing owner commitments first.
3. Clear invariant vs variable rules for designers/editors (handoff visual prompts to `09-image-video` and final copy polishing to `10-ad-copy` / `10-copy-editing`), with mobile crop/safe-zone verification noted before media upload.
4. Explicit draft status requiring owner approval prior to production spend or live launch.
