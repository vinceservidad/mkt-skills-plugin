# Creative testing guide

## Layers

| Layer | What changes | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concept | The idea, angle or format | Comparison with supermarket beans vs a customer review vs a brewing demo |
| Variation | Hook, opening, visual within a concept | Three openings for the comparison video |
| Iteration | Small edits | Caption length, thumbnail |

Six captions on the same photo is one concept with six variations.

## Fair read

Platforms shift spend to early favourites; that is a delivery decision, not proof. Give each concept a minimum spend or a target number of outcomes before judging, using separate ad sets or the platform's test tool (cite current docs).

## Outcome

Cost per new customer in a stated attribution view, with a quality guardrail. Clicks and CTR are signals.

## Fatigue

Rising frequency and cost with falling results; plan replacements before it happens.
