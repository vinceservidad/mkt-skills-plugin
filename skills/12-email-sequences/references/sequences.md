# Sequence types

| Sequence | Emails | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soap Opera (Brunson) | 5 | 1 set the stage, 2 high drama (backstory), 3 epiphany, 4 hidden benefits, 5 urgency and call to action |
| Seinfeld emails (Brunson) | daily | a daily broadcast of short story-led emails after the Soap Opera Sequence; needs consent and an owner-approved frequency |
| Welcome | 3–5 | deliver the magnet → quick win → story → proof → offer |
| Nurture | 4–8 | one useful idea per email, with soft calls to action, moving one awareness level |
| Launch | 6–10 | pre-launch content → open cart → proof → objections → close (only real deadlines) |
| Sales or follow-up after a call or webinar | 3–6 | replay → objection answers → proof → deadline (if real) |
| Lead follow-up | 3–5 | for leads who booked nothing: one question, one proof, one offer |

## Rules

- Stop the sequence when the goal is met: stop selling to buyers.
- Subject lines are honest and specific.
- Every deadline, discount and capacity claim exists in `offer.md`.
