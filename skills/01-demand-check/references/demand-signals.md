# Demand signals and their limits

| Signal | What it shows | What it does not show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estimated monthly searches | Relative interest in a term, for the tool's location, language and period | The number of buyers; the share who would pay; demand from people who don't search |
| Competition label and bid range | That advertisers compete for the term, and the tool's estimate of what they pay | Whether advertisers profit; your cost per customer |
| Trend label | The tool's direction over its period | Why it moved; whether it will continue |
| Ads seen on a search or in an ad library | Who advertised, with what message, on that date | Spend, reach, performance, or whether it works. Presence is not spend |
| Count of providers | How crowded the supply is | Whether they are profitable or growing |
| Price bands | What providers ask, on the date observed | What buyers actually pay after discounts; willingness to pay |
| Own leads by source, search-console queries, waitlists | Real demand reaching this business | Demand going to competitors |

## Intent groups

| Group | Example | Usually means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solution, specific | *bookkeeping for creative agencies* | Ready buyers, small volume, high value |
| Solution, generic | *online bookkeeping services* | Mixed buyers, mostly not this ICP; expensive to capture |
| Problem or DIY | *cash flow forecast template* | Problem-aware people solving it themselves; education, lead magnets |
| Alternative | *fractional CFO for agencies* | Buyers weighing a different solution; comparison content |

Report the business's own demand as the specific group plus the part of other groups the evidence ties to its buyer. Never add generic volume to the business's demand without saying so.

## Verdicts

- **Existing, capturable:** specific terms have volume, and advertisers compete for them.
- **Existing as pain, not as search:** customer research shows the problem, but buyers don't search for the solution. Route: referrals, communities, content on the problem terms.
- **To be created:** little search, few ads, few providers, buyers don't name the category. Creating demand is slow and expensive; say so plainly, and hand category work to `06-category-design`.
- **Unknown:** the evidence is too thin. Say what to collect.

## Calculations

- Group total = sum of the group's terms' estimated searches (show the terms).
- Share = specific total ÷ (specific + generic) — only as a rough indication; volumes are estimates.
- Never convert searches into customers or revenue without a measured click and conversion rate from this business. If you show a scenario, label every rate `(assumption)`.

## Platform currency

Keyword tools, search engines and ad libraries change what they show and how they estimate. State only what the export or the dated observation shows. Any statement about how a tool currently works needs the platform's own documentation, cited with its URL and the date checked; otherwise mark it *unverified*.
