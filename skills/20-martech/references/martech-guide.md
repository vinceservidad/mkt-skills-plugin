# Martech guide

## Inventory fields

Tool · job · monthly cost · owner · used or unused · connects to.

## Flows to trace

Sign-up and consent · purchase · cancellation and pause · refund · attribution (UTMs, pixels).

## Breaks

A record that changes in one tool and not another (for example a cancellation that never reaches email).

## Choosing tools

Process first, requirements second, vendor last; cite vendor facts with a date.
