# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Martech stack

The set of marketing tools and how they connect. Applied as an inventory with jobs, costs, owners and data flows.

## CRM

Customer relationship management: one record per customer or lead with history. Applied when a process needs it, not before.

## Marketing automation

Rules that send messages or update records on events. Applied to flows that are traced and owned.

## RevOps

One owner for data and connections across marketing, sales and support. Applied to name owners per flow.
