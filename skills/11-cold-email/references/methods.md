# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

Treat a short list of named accounts as markets of one: choose them on fit, research a dated reason to reach each one, and coordinate the touches to the people who decide. Here it sets the tiers: one-to-one emails for the best-fit accounts with a real signal, a shared angle for the rest.

## Cold outreach

Contacting people who have not asked to hear from you, usually by email or LinkedIn. Applied as list, first line, value, soft ask and follow-up, sized to capacity, sent at a volume the domain can carry, and judged on qualified conversations.
