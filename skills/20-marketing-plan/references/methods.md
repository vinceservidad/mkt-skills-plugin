# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Marketing plan

One page: outcome, priorities, owners, budget, measures, calendar. Applied from the constraint and the team's capacity.

## OKRs

Objectives with measurable key results. Applied with baselines from the source of truth and owner-set targets.

## RACI

Responsible, accountable, consulted, informed. Applied with one accountable owner per priority.

## EOS / Scaling Up

Operating rhythms (weekly meeting, quarterly priorities, scorecard). Applied as the plan's review rhythm.

## T-shaped marketer / pod structure

A generalist with one deep skill; small cross-functional pods. Applied to describe the team shape as it grows.
