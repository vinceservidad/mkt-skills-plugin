# Marketing plan guide

## One page

Outcome and baseline · 3 priorities and not-now list · OKRs · owners · capacity and budget · rhythm · calendar.

## OKRs

Objective (qualitative) → 2 or 3 key results (number, baseline, target set by the owner, source).

## RACI

One A per priority. R does the work; C is asked; I is told.

## Rhythm

Weekly 30-minute meeting on numbers and blockers; monthly scorecard; quarterly reset of priorities.

## Capacity

Hours per week per person; a plan needing more hours than exist is a wish list.
