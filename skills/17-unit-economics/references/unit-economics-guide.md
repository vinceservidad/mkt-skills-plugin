# Unit economics guide

## Contribution margin

Per order: price − product − fulfilment − shipping − payment fees − refunds (unless net revenue already excludes them). Label each input.

## LTV

Contribution per customer over the observed window = contribution per order × orders per starting customer in the window (from cohorts). Extrapolations (for example assume the box-3-to-4 retention continues) are labelled scenarios.

## CAC

Blended = all marketing spend ÷ new customers (system of record), same period. Channel CAC needs an attribution view; say which.

## Payback

CAC ÷ contribution per billing period. Payback later than the observed retention window is unproven.

## Ratios

MER = total revenue ÷ total marketing spend. Break-even ROAS on first order = 1 ÷ (contribution ÷ revenue on that order).

## Traps

Revenue LTV instead of contribution; platform conversions as customers; counting discounts twice; calling contribution "profit" without naming the level.
