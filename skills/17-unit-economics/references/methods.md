# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Unit economics

The revenue and cost of one unit (an order or a customer). Applied as contribution margin per order with labelled inputs and the profit level named.

## CAC / LTV / payback

Cost to acquire a customer, the contribution a customer brings over time, and how long until it covers CAC. Applied with observed retention and source-system customer counts.

## MER

Marketing efficiency ratio: total revenue ÷ total marketing spend. Applied as the blended health check that no attribution model can game.

## ROAS

Return on ad spend: revenue attributed to ads ÷ ad spend, in a platform's or a model's view. Applied against break-even ROAS, never as profit.
