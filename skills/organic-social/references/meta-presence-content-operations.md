## Source basis

ASSOC-2026-01 pages 10-19 discuss Page information, content, insights, community and access; pages 21-29 discuss Instagram profiles, formats, engagement and creative recommendations. Page 46 describes planning, inbox and insights in Business Suite. [Source identity and dated-feature caveats](../../meta-ads/references/meta-2026-guides-source-notes.md).

The guide supplies starting options, not proof that a cadence or format improves this account. The operating brief below is an original MKT application of the existing organic-social method. It does not claim a current ranking rule or replace platform-native checks.

## Presence readiness

Check the supplied profile's business name, description, category, imagery, hours/contact information and intended action/destination against verified business facts. Identify what is missing and draft the corrected field brief with `$copywriting`. Do not infer ownership or task permissions from seeing a public Page. Verify current fields and account type when implementation depends on them.

A profile should tell a visitor what the business offers and what to do next. Do not fabricate awards, service areas, opening hours, reviews or contact details to make it look complete. Treat shops, product tags, format limits and scheduling options in the guide as edition descriptions until the account confirms current availability.

## Content-to-community plan

Use actual customer questions and supplied observations, not invented voice-of-customer quotes. Select a communication job for each candidate: demonstrate a product, explain a process, introduce the team, answer a question or support a community discussion. A source category is an option, not a quota.

For each selected piece return `audience situation | evidence | single idea | format hypothesis | required assets | copy brief | next action | response owner | success signal | status`.

Develop the requested work rather than return headings alone. A small plan can contain a product-use demonstration with a verified factual claim, a process explanation supported by real production evidence, and a sourced FAQ response. `$copywriting` produces the exact captions or profile text in the same coordinated task. If the evidence supports only one piece, say which missing assets block the others rather than inventing them.

Choose a sustainable cadence from actual asset and moderation capacity. The source recommends regular posting and timely replies; it does not establish that a specific weekly count, time-of-day or reply target is feasible or causally improves distribution for this account. Use [cadence and community management](cadence-and-community-management.md) for the operating constraints.

For comments, mentions and messages, define responsibility, expected response time, escalation and a private-support path for sensitive customer details. Record recurring questions for future content without publishing private conversations as testimonials. A positive response is not consent to use someone's words or likeness in an ad.

## Learn without overstating the result

Compare like-for-like content with its context, period, format and distribution. Reach, interactions and follower changes describe audience response; a sales claim requires an actual link/code/source connection and still does not establish incrementality. One high-performing post is a hypothesis for replication, not a permanent content pillar.

An organic candidate can inform paid creative, but `$meta-ads` owns whether to boost it and `$creative-strategy` owns the paid concept adaptation. Do not spend from this reference. A content calendar is a draft artifact, not a scheduled queue; any recurring execution needs the existing operations controls.

**Return:** presence gaps with factual corrections, completed content briefs and coordinated copy, realistic production/moderation plan, measurement limitations, next learning question and exact status. No account, post or publishing schedule has been changed by retrieving this method.
