# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Marketplace & retail media

Selling and advertising inside commerce marketplaces such as Amazon, TikTok Shop, Walmart and Etsy. Applied as post-fee contribution margin and break-even ACoS checks first, hero SKU selection, passing retail readiness gates (Buy Box, listing SEO, images, stock depth, reviews) before ad spend, separating branded and non-branded retail media campaigns, and judging performance on TACoS and net profit.
