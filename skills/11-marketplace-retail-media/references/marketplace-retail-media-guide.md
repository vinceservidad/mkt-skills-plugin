# Marketplace and retail media guide

## 1. Why marketplaces behave differently from DTC

- **Closed intent + flywheel:** Paid clicks that convert raise sales velocity, which improves organic marketplace rank — so organic and paid are coupled.
- **Margin stack:** Marketplace revenue carries referral fees, fulfillment/storage fees (e.g., FBA or TikTok Shop shipping subsidies/SLAs), returns/damages and ad spend. Never copy a DTC ROAS target onto Amazon or TikTok Shop.
- **Customer ownership:** Marketplaces restrict off-platform solicitation (for example diverting Amazon buyers to a DTC website via inserts or messages violates Amazon policy — verify current Seller Central policy). Use marketplaces as a profitable acquisition/discovery channel, not a policy-violating funnel hack.

## 2. Unit economics: ACoS vs. TACoS vs. Break-Even ACoS

- **Break-even ACoS** = `(Selling Price - COGS - Referral Fee - Fulfillment Fee - Return Allowance) / Selling Price`.
  - Spending above break-even ACoS loses money on the first order (only defensible temporarily during a deliberate rank launch with a fixed cap and stop rule).
- **ACoS** = `Ad Spend / Ad-Attributed Sales` (efficiency of the ad campaigns alone).
- **TACoS** = `Ad Spend / Total Marketplace Sales (Organic + Paid)` (measures whether ads are growing organic rank over time; healthy launches often start with higher ACoS/TACoS, then TACoS falls as organic sales climb).

## 3. Retail readiness checklist (all 6 before spending)

1. **Buy Box / Featured Offer ownership** (never pay for ads when another seller or suppressed pricing owns the Buy Box).
2. **In-stock depth & fulfillment speed** (running out of stock collapses organic velocity; keep buffer above lead time).
3. **Search-indexed listing SEO:** Title leads with primary product + high-intent modifier, bullets answer top buyer objections/uses from VoC, backend search terms cover synonyms without repetition.
4. **Main image & visual stack:** High-contrast main image for search-grid CTR + benefit callouts, comparison chart and A+ content for conversion.
5. **Review floor:** Launch with compliant review programs (e.g., Amazon Vine where eligible — verify current fee/rules; never incentivize positive reviews).
6. **Price parity & packaging:** Watch cross-channel price-parity policies (Buy Box suppression if DTC is materially cheaper) and shelf-life/packaging rules for consumables.

## 4. Amazon Ads architecture

- **Discovery vs. Performance separation:**
  - *Auto / Broad / Phrase campaigns:* Low-to-moderate bids to mine converting search terms and ASINs.
  - *Exact Match / Product Targeting campaigns:* Promote proven search terms and high-converting competitor ASINs; add promoted terms as exact negatives in discovery campaigns to prevent self-bidding.
- **Brand vs. Non-Brand isolation:** Always separate branded keywords (your brand name) into their own campaign with strict spend caps so cheap brand clicks never mask unprofitable non-brand discovery.
- **Ad layers:** Start with Sponsored Products (highest buyer intent) → add Sponsored Brands (category/storefront discovery, Brand Registry required) → add Sponsored Display (ASIN defense & retargeting).

## 5. TikTok Shop & creator retail media

- **Three levers working together:** Shop listing SEO + Creator Affiliate network (Open Collaboration for broad discovery + Target Collaboration for vetted creators, with sample seeding) + Shop Ads / Video Shopping Ads / LIVE Shopping Ads.
- **Strict fulfillment SLAs:** TikTok Shop enforces dispatch and tracking SLAs and Shop Performance Score (SPS) penalties; confirm operational readiness before scaling creator volume.
- **Margin check:** Account for platform referral fee + creator commission % + free-sample cost + shipping subsidy + ad spend in one unit-economics sheet.

## 6. Platform facts rule

Marketplace referral fees, FBA fees, Vine fees, Brand Registry rules, TikTok Shop commission caps/SLAs and Walmart/Etsy ad formats change frequently. Always verify against current official Seller Central / TikTok Shop Academy / Walmart Seller docs (cite URL + date) or mark *to confirm*.
