# Temperature matching

| Temperature | Awareness | Typical sources | Page | Offer | Call to action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold | unaware, problem aware | paid social to new audiences, sponsorships, display, broad search on symptoms | content page, story, lead-magnet or opt-in page | free value: checklist, guide, quiz, template, video | "Get the…", "Take the quiz" |
| Warm | problem aware, solution aware | email list, podcast listeners, content readers, social followers, retargeting of content viewers, category search | case study, comparison, demo or sample page | demo, sample with their data, trial, low-ticket offer | "See how it works", "Book a demo" |
| Hot | product aware, most aware | referrals, branded search, returning visitors, retargeting of pricing or checkout visitors, existing customers | offer page, pricing page, checkout, booking | the core offer, with its guarantee | "Start now", "Book your call", "Buy" |

## Signals that correct a label

- **Conversion by source:** a source that converts several times better than the rest is hotter than it looks.
- **The first question asked:** "What is this?" means cold; "How much?" means hot.
- **The path taken:** visitors from a source who read the pricing page first are usually hot.

## Common mismatches

- Cold ads sent to a "Book a call" page: high cost per lead and low show rates.
- Hot referrals sent to a generic home page or a free guide: deals delayed or lost.
- One landing page for every source: it fits none of them.
- No warming path: cold visitors who don't buy are never seen again.
