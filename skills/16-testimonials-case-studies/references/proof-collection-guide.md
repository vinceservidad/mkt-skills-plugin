# Proof collection guide

## Interview questions

1. What was going on before? What did it cost you?
2. What almost stopped you from choosing us?
3. Why did you choose us over the alternatives?
4. What happened in the first weeks and months?
5. What changed, in numbers if you have them?
6. What would you tell a peer who is unsure?

Record exact words. Ask permission to record.

## Structure

Before → doubt → choice → after → specific result → their words to a peer.

## Verification

Check every number against records; send the draft to the customer to approve. If they won't sign off on a number, use their words without it.

## Permission

Written consent covering name, company, logo, photo, numbers, and where it appears. Offer anonymity (role and size only).

## Rules

Endorsement rules (in the US, the FTC Endorsement Guides) require that testimonials reflect honest experience and that atypical results are disclosed; verify current rules or mark *to confirm*.
