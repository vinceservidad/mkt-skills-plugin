# Analytics guide

## Funnel

Conversion at each step = count at step ÷ count at previous step. Report both: the largest relative drop and the largest absolute loss of people. Segment by device, source, new versus returning.

## Cohorts

Group by start period; measure retention at the same step since start (box 2, week 4). A recent cohort with fewer steps is not worse, just younger.

## Caution

- Small counts: say so; a 2-point change on 50 people is noise.
- Different windows or definitions break comparisons.
- Tracking defects (see 17-tracking-plan) can create fake patterns.
- Correlation is not cause; say *associated with*, and test.

## Language

Observed (computed), inferred (labelled), unknown (what the data can't show).
