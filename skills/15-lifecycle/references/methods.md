# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Lifecycle marketing

Messages triggered by a customer's stage (new, active, repeat, at risk, lapsed). Applied as stages with data rules, one flow per stage that matters, and measurement against a holdout.

## Customer marketing

Marketing to existing customers to deepen use and introduce the next offer. Applied to customers whose behaviour shows readiness for the next step on the Value ladder.

## Push notifications

Messages from an app to the phone's lock screen. Applied only with the app's notification permission, for timely service messages first, with an opt-out.

## RFM

Recency, Frequency, Monetary: segmenting customers by how recently, how often and how much they buy. Applied where order data allows, to decide who gets which flow.

## Win-back

Bringing back customers who lapsed or cancelled. Applied by addressing the reason they left (delivery, cadence, taste) before any discount, and only to customers who agreed to marketing.
