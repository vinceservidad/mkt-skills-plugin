# Lifecycle guide

## Stages

| Stage | Example rule | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | First order in the last 14 days | Help using it; set expectations |
| Active | Ordered or subscribed and not at risk | Value, not noise |
| Repeat | Several orders | Recognition; next step if ready |
| At risk | Pausing, skipping, a late delivery, a complaint | Fix the reason |
| Lapsed or cancelled | No order or subscription | Win-back on the reason they left |

## RFM

Score recency, frequency and monetary value from order history. Champions (recent, frequent, high) get recognition and referral asks; at-risk high-value customers get personal attention; new customers get onboarding.

## Win-back

Lead with the fix for the reason they left (cadence, delivery, taste, price fit). A discount is an owner decision with a cost and an end date. Only to people who agreed to marketing on that channel.

## Channels

Email for most flows; SMS for time-sensitive service messages with consent (see 11-sms); push for app users who allowed notifications. Rules differ by country and channel; verify or mark *to confirm*.

## Measure

Hold out a random share of each flow's audience, or compare equal periods. Report retention, repeat orders, revenue per customer and unsubscribes.
