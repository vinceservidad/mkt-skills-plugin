## Fascinations

Shape: a specific benefit plus curiosity. For example: *The one number that tells you on a Monday whether you can afford Friday's hire (page 2 of the report)*.

Every fascination must be paid off by the offer.

## QUEST (Fortin)

Qualify, Understand, Educate, Stimulate, Transition. A good alternative for solution-aware, B2B readers.
