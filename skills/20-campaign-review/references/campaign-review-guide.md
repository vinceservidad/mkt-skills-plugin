# Campaign review guide

## Structure

Goal vs result · timeline (planned vs actual) · what happened (facts) · why (evidence) · guesses (labelled) · keep, change, stop · actions.

## Blameless

Write "the page went live before stock was loaded", not who did it. Fix the process.

## Weekly growth meeting (30 minutes)

1. Numbers against plan. 2. Last week's actions. 3. What shipped and what it did. 4. Blockers. 5. Next week's priorities and owners.
