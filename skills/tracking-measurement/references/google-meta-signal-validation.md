## MKT application

Produce a reconciliation ledger: actual business action and status, browser/server representation, platform receipt, deduplication outcome, attribution credit, reporting date basis and business-record match. For each stage, distinguish observed, calculated and unknown; receiving an event does not prove its value or eligibility is correct.

Use consented synthetic fixtures for proposed tests. Verify duplicates, failed or refunded outcomes, currency/value, late arrival, retries and withdrawn consent using the existing measurement method. Fetch current official technical specifications before prescribing provider fields or exact deduplication behavior; the course overview does not establish them.

**Synthetic reasoning check:** twenty paid orders represented once in browser data and once in server data remain twenty business orders, not forty. Diagnose representation/collection duplication before choosing a bid or making a performance claim. Google and Meta attribution can overlap; never add their credited sales as deduplicated business revenue.

Define the value basis before a return calculation. A GBP 1,000 spend and GBP 4,000 attributed conversion value gives 4.0x attributed value/spend, not proof of profit. Commercial decisions need the specified reconciled revenue and costs. Distinguish a collection defect, legitimate attribution difference, maturity lag and causal question instead of forcing every report total to match.

**Deliverable:** populated ledger, verified defects versus expected discrepancies, bounded repair/validation plan, downstream decisions blocked or supported and exact status. Preserve source privacy, separate test and production data, and obtain approval before changing events, goals, values, tags or optimization signals. A documented method does not make a connected account available.
