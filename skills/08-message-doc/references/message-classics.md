# Messaging classics (Ogilvy & Schwartz)

Use these two classic message-design lenses after reading upstream offer, customer, and awareness evidence. Keep every competitor note, number, and customer claim strictly traceable.

## 1. Big Idea 5-question test (Ogilvy)

From David Ogilvy (*Ogilvy on Advertising*), score each candidate Big Idea in `context/message.md` on all five questions before picking one winner:

1. **Did it make me gasp when I first saw it?** Does it connect the buyer's real pain and the product's mechanism in an unexpected, plain way rather than a category cliche?
2. **Do I wish I had thought of it myself?** Does it feel unmistakably right for this specific business?
3. **Is it unique?** Check `context/market.md`: would the primary competitor plausibly say the same sentence? If yes, reject it or mark the gap to verify.
4. **Does it fit the strategy to perfection?** Does it match the awareness level (`03-awareness-map`), target customer (`06-positioning-statement`), and core offer (`07-offer-design`)?
5. **Could it run for 30 years?** Avoid disposable trends or seasonal wordplay that lock the message out of long-term campaigns.

## 2. Intensification and copy mechanics (Schwartz)

From Eugene Schwartz (*Breakthrough Advertising*), plan how downstream copy will prove and intensify desire inside `## Message house` without inventing claims:

- **Channel mass desire, do not try to create it:** anchor the Roof promise in a desire or frustration buyers already express in Voice of Customer records.
- **Gradualization:** move the reader step by step from an undeniable everyday truth they already accept (`Awareness map`) toward the business's bigger promise and mechanism.
- **Redefinition:** simplify a complex or intimidating solution into clear steps or show what the mechanism unlocks cleanly without jargon.
- **Mechanization:** explain *how* the product works concretely inside `## Mechanism` and `## Reason why` so the claim feels physical and believable.
- **Concentration:** compress the proof into concrete specifics (exact steps, verified client counts, real turnaround windows from `offer.md`) rather than abstract adjectives.
