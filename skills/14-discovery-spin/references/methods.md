# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## SPIN Selling

Rackham's question sequence: Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff. Applied with few situation questions and most time on implication and payoff, in the buyer's words.

## Gap Selling

Keenan: the sale is the gap between the buyer's current state and future state; the seller's job is to make that gap clear and sized. Applied as the gap in the buyer's own numbers.

## MEDDIC / MEDDPICC

A qualification checklist: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, (Paper process), Identify pain, Champion, (Competition). Applied for multi-person B2B deals; each field heard or unknown.
