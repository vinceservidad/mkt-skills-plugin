# Discovery guide

## SPIN

| Type | Purpose | Example shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | Facts you can't look up | How do you track cash today? |
| Problem | Surface the difficulty | Where does that break down? |
| Implication | Size the cost | What happened the last time that came late? What did it cost? |
| Need-payoff | Let them state the value | If you knew that on Monday, what would you decide differently? |

Ask few situation questions; most value is in implication and need-payoff.

## Gap

Current state (facts, problems, impact, root cause) → future state (what good looks like, impact). The gap is the difference, in their numbers. The buyer, not the seller, says it.

## MEDDIC and MEDDPICC

Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process (contracts, procurement), Identify pain, Champion, Competition. Use the fields that matter for the deal size. A deal with no known economic buyer is not qualified.

## Deciders

Ask early: who else will weigh in, how do you usually decide on something like this, what would they need to see. Book the person who signs rather than proposing around them.

## Never

Leading questions that answer themselves, guessing a field, or continuing past a clear disqualifier.
