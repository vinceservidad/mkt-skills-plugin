# Podcast and video guide

## Guest or own

| Question | Guest first | Own show or channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the business have an audience? | No | Yes, or willing to build for months |
| Founder hours a week | A few hours a month | Enough for a steady cadence plus editing |
| Evidence | Past guest spots brought leads | Topics people search for or ask about repeatedly |

A channel that starts at three videos a week and stops in month two is worse than one a month that runs for a year.

## Picking shows

Fit (do buyers listen? from discovery calls, lead sources, interviews), reachability (a host who had the founder on, a show that takes guests), topic match. Show size is the host's claim.

## The pitch

Short: who the founder is in one line, one specific topic for this show's listeners, what they will learn, a past episode link if one exists, and availability. One follow-up.

## Episodes

Teach one thing the buyer needs. Use the founder's own story. Client stories only with permission. Close with one next step that can be counted (a page with a unique link, a resource that exists).

## YouTube specifics (verify current features)

Titles built on what buyers search; thumbnails that state the outcome; chapters; descriptions with the next step. Platform features (Shorts, chapters, end screens, analytics fields) change: cite YouTube Help pages with URL and date, or mark *to confirm*.

## Repurposing

Each episode gives clips for social, a short written summary for the newsletter, and quotes from the founder (never from guests without permission).

## Measure

Leads and clients per show or video, from a source field plus a unique link. Listens and views are signals; platforms count them differently.
