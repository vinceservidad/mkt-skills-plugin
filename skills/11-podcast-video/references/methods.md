# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Podcast marketing / guesting

Appearing on other people's podcasts, or running one. Applied as guesting first on shows the buyer follows, topics from the buyer's jobs, one next step per episode, and leads counted per show.

## YouTube / video marketing

Publishing video, often on YouTube, that buyers find by search or recommendation. Applied only at a cadence the owner can keep, built on searchable jobs, repurposed into clips, and judged on leads and clients.
