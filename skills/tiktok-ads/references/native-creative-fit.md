# Native Creative Fit

A core creative question on TikTok is whether an asset reads as native to the feed or as an obvious ad. Treat the effect of native fit on distribution and watch-through as a hypothesis to test in the account, not as documented platform behavior; confirm any current platform guidance per [`PLATFORM-CURRENCY.md`](../../../library/contracts/PLATFORM-CURRENCY.md).

## What "native" means here

Vertical, full-screen, shot to look like organic creator content — handheld camera feel, on-screen text or captions in the platform's visual language, sound or trending audio used the way organic creators use it, and a message delivered the way a person would say it rather than the way a brand would write it. Production polish (professional lighting, studio sets, scripted voiceover) is not disqualifying; whether it costs attention in this account is part of what the test measures.

## Assessing available creative

1. Ask whether the asset was produced for TikTok specifically or repurposed from another platform. Repurposed, vertically cropped horizontal video is a visible tell; whether viewers discount it in this account is a hypothesis to test, not a documented platform effect.
2. Ask whether the opening one to two seconds work as a native hook — a question, a visual pattern interrupt, or a claim stated the way a creator would state it — rather than a logo or brand card; whether a brand-first opening loses viewers here is testable from the account's own watch-time or retention reporting where it is available.
3. Ask whether genuine creator or customer content is available or producible with the rights and disclosure it requires. Test it as a hypothesis; never script content to pass as an independent customer or creator when it is not.
4. Ask whether the audio is native to the platform (trending sound, native voiceover) rather than a licensed brand jingle or a track that reads as a commercial.

## Rules

- Do not default to reformatting an existing high-production asset for TikTok without assessing whether its production quality itself is the mismatch; a purpose-built asset is a hypothesis worth testing against the repurposed one, and recommending "polish it more" without that check can make the mismatch worse.
- Route concept, hook, and angle development to `$creative-strategy`; this reference assesses fit to the platform's native format, it does not develop the underlying creative concept.
- Test native-style and higher-production creative against each other rather than asserting one wins by default; the fit varies by category and audience, and a categorical claim without account-specific evidence should be treated as a hypothesis.
- A single high-performing native asset does not establish a durable creative pattern; require replication before generalizing "this style works" into a standing creative brief.
