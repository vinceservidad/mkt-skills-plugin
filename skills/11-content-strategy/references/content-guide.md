# Content strategy: decision rules

## Mission and tilt (Pulizzi)

A content mission names the audience, what they will get and the outcome for them. The *content tilt* is the angle the business can own: real expertise, a real story, data it holds, a method it actually uses. Take it from `offer.md`, `message.md` (Big idea, Mechanism, Story) or the founder's notes. If none exists yet, say so; do not invent a credential.

## Pillar tests

A pillar passes when all four hold:

1. **Evidenced:** a pain, job, belief or question that appears in the customer evidence (cite IDs and counts as they are written).
2. **Ownable:** the business can speak to it with real expertise or data.
3. **Leads somewhere:** solving it points to the core offer (or to a free step on the value ladder).
4. **Right reader:** it attracts the ICP, not the segments the ICP disqualifies. A topic that draws enthusiasts or micro-businesses the offer is not for is a rejected pillar, with the reason.

Two to four pillars. More dilutes the tilt; one is fine when the evidence points to one.

## Cluster fields

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Topic | The buyer's question, phrased as they phrase it (paraphrase in plain text; quotation marks only for exact source words) |
| Journey stage / awareness level | See, Think, Do, Care (or the map's stages); Unaware to Most aware |
| Evidence | The IDs the question comes from |
| Search data | Only from a supplied export, as written, with tool and date; otherwise blank |
| Format | Article, guide, template, episode, carousel, short video, email |
| Next step | A free asset or offer that exists, or *gap: needs …* with its owner skill (`12-lead-magnet`, `07-value-ladder`) |

Early-stage topics (unaware, problem aware) name the problem in the buyer's words and teach; later-stage topics (solution and product aware) compare, demonstrate and answer objections. Do not put a hard sell on an unaware topic.

## Search data rule

Search volumes, difficulty and trends are tool estimates. Use only figures from a supplied export, quoted as written, with the tool's date. Never fill a missing volume. Search-engine behaviour (ranking factors, AI answers, featured snippets) is a platform claim: cite the search engine's own documentation with a date or mark *to confirm*, and hand the work to `11-seo` or `11-ai-seo-geo`.

## Repurposing (one to many)

Start from what exists (podcast episodes, webinars, sales-call answers, the founder's story). One pillar asset becomes:

- a long piece (article or guide) that is the canonical home;
- 3 to 5 short derivatives per channel (posts, a carousel, a short video, a newsletter section);
- an answer the sales team can send.

Each derivative is native to its channel; a channel's format specifics are platform claims to confirm.

## Distribution and capture (rent to own)

For each piece, name the route:

- **Owned:** email list, website, community.
- **Earned:** guest spots, podcasts, partners' newsletters, press (`11-pr`).
- **Paid:** promotion of the piece (hand to `11-paid-social` or `paid-search`).

Every pillar ends at a capture point the business owns: a lead magnet or newsletter sign-up. Attention on someone else's platform is rented until it becomes an email address.

## Cadence and capacity

A sustainable cadence beats a burst followed by silence (heuristic). Set it from the hours and people stated in `inputs/`. When capacity is unknown, propose a minimal cadence as an assumption and ask: who writes, who edits, who designs, how many hours a week, what weeks are blocked.

## Measurement

| Stage | Signal (not the outcome) | Outcome metric | Attribution method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See / unaware | Reach, views | New subscribers | Sign-up source, UTM |
| Think / problem and solution aware | Time on page, returning readers | Lead-magnet downloads, sample requests | UTM to CRM |
| Do / product aware | Page visits from content | Calls or orders sourced | CRM source field |
| Care | Opens | Referrals, retention | Referral source |

If no attribution method exists, say so and hand it to `17-tracking-plan`.
