# Compliance guide

## Claims

Superlatives ("freshest", "best"), numbers ("10,000 customers"), and process claims ("roasted within 24 hours") need evidence you could show.

## Prices and urgency

A "was" price must be a price actually charged; a timer must end when it says; scarcity must be real.

## Consent

Opt-ins not pre-ticked; separate consent per channel where rules require; honour unsubscribes and cancellations across tools.

## Dark patterns

Hard-to-cancel flows, pre-ticked boxes, fake countdowns, confirmshaming, hidden fees.

## Rules

Consumer-protection, privacy and messaging rules vary by place and change; cite the current source with a date or mark to confirm with a lawyer.
