# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Compliance checklist

A review of claims, prices, urgency, consent, privacy and platform policies. Applied item by item with evidence, cited rules and fixes.

## Consent and privacy

Opt-ins freely given, data use disclosed, choices honoured. Applied to forms, cookies, pixels and suppression flows.

## Dark patterns (what not to do)

Design that tricks people: hard cancellation, pre-ticked boxes, fake timers, false reference prices, confirmshaming. Applied by naming each and its fix.
