# Loyalty guide

## Types

| Type | Best when | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surprise and delight | Small business, close customers | Keep it occasional and genuine |
| Milestone reward | A known drop-off point | Place it before the drop |
| Points | Frequent purchases of varied size | Cost, liability, complexity |
| Tiers | Wide spread of spend | Few customers reach top tiers |
| Paid membership | Clear member value | Must be worth more than it costs |

## Cost

Effective reward rate = reward cost ÷ spend needed to earn it. Compare with margin per order: a reward of $13.60 cost (one bag delivered) for $500 spend is 2.7% of revenue and about 7% of the margin on that spend (about 22.7 bags × $8.40 ≈ $191) if margin is $8.40 per $22 bag. Breakage (unredeemed points) lowers the cost but is an assumption until measured.

## Rules

Earn rule, reward, eligibility, expiry, changes with notice. Plain words. Verify consumer-law rules for the country or mark *to confirm*.

## Lift

Members versus a holdout or matched non-members, over the window that matters. Programs often reward people who would buy anyway; only extra behaviour pays for the program.
