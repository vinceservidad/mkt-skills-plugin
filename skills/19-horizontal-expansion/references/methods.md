# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Ansoff Matrix

Four growth paths: market penetration, product development, market development, diversification. Applied to place each option.

## Core–Adjacent–Transformational

Nagji and Tuff's distance from the core. Applied so closer, better-evidenced options go first.

## Crossing the Chasm

Moore's point that a new segment can buy for different reasons and needs a whole product and a beachhead. Applied to new-segment options.

## Horizontal scale paths

New segments, offers, channels or markets next to a stable core. Applied with demand evidence, needs, capacity, cost, a small test and a stop rule.
