# Expansion guide

## Core first

Retention steady, delivery reliable, constraint not in the core. Otherwise fix the core.

## Ansoff

| | Existing products | New products |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing markets | Penetration | Product development |
| New markets | Market development | Diversification |

## Distance

Core (same buyers, same offer), adjacent (one thing new), transformational (both new).

## Chasm

A new segment may need different proof, product and channel; pick a beachhead.

## Test

Smallest test (waitlist, pilot with a few accounts, one region) with a stop rule and a cost cap.
