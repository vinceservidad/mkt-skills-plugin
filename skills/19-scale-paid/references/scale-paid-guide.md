# Scaling paid media guide

## Readiness gates

| Gate | Pass when |
| --- | --- |
| Economics | Contribution margin and payback allow the target cost per customer |
| Measurement | Platform counts reconciled with store or CRM; defects fixed or adjusted |
| Constraint | Acquisition limits the outcome, not retention or capacity |
| Capacity | Fulfilment and team can absorb the volume |
| Creative | Enough distinct concepts to feed more spend |

## Marginal cost

Compare spend and new customers (source of truth) month by month; the cost of the next customer is usually higher than the average.

## Steps

Owner-set increments, each held long enough to read on the business outcome, with a guardrail and roll-back rule. No overnight doubling.

## Signals of saturation

Rising frequency and CPM, falling new customers per dollar, same ads for months.
