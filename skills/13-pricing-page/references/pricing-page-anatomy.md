# Pricing page anatomy: sections, anchors, decoys, what may be published

Anchoring and decoy effects are documented in behavioural research (Kahneman; Ariely). On a pricing page they are **tactics**, useful only when honest; they do not prove a price is right or that a layout will convert. The page's own numbers and tests decide.

## What may be published

| In `offer.md` | On the page? |
| --- | --- |
| Price drafted by `07-pricing` with evidence | Yes, as a draft for the owner to confirm before publishing |
| Price or deal marked `(assumption)`, *not yet offered*, *proposed*, *not set* | No. Open questions |
| Guarantee drafted by `07-guarantee-risk-reversal` | Only with *owner to approve wording*; clauses that depend on something not built are held |
| Capacity limit stated as true | Yes, stated plainly |
| A bonus listed as proposed | No |

## Sections

1. **Headline:** what the buyer gets for the price, in their words, not "Pricing".
2. **Plans or bands:** the value metric that sets the price (revenue band, bags and frequency, seats) explained in a line.
3. **What each includes:** from the Core offer, existing items only.
4. **Anchor:** the alternative the buyer already compares (a hire, a higher-priced option, the cost of the problem), with a sourced figure.
5. **Recommended plan:** only if evidence shows most buyers choose or fit it.
6. **Risk reversal:** guarantee, cancellation, renewal terms that exist.
7. **FAQ:** price and risk objections from the Objection map.
8. **Next step:** one call to action per plan, and what happens after.

## Show prices or "contact us"?

Evidence for showing: buyers ask about price on calls, deals are lost on price after a call, the price is set by a simple rule (a band) before the call, cheaper alternatives publish theirs. Evidence for not showing: the price depends on scoping that only a conversation can do. A band or "from" price is a middle path. The decision is the owner's.

## Decoys and anchors: honest only

- A decoy is a plan placed to make another look better. Use one only if it is a real plan the business would happily sell at that price.
- No crossed-out "was" prices that were never charged.
- No competitor prices without a dated source.
- No "most popular" label without evidence.

## Whole price

Show shipping, fees and taxes or say when they apply. A free-shipping or free-trial claim carries its condition next to it (*free shipping on subscriptions*).

## Metrics

Primary: paid orders or qualified calls from the page, by plan or band. Supporting: clicks per plan. Guardrails: refunds, wrong-band or wrong-fit calls, support questions about price. Price changes themselves are tested by `18-price-testing`, not by the page layout.
