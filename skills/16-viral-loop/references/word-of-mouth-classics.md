# Word-of-mouth classics (Godin & Barnum)

Pair Seth Godin's *Unleashing the IdeaVirus* / *Purple Cow* and P.T. Barnum's talkability mechanics with Jonah Berger's STEPPS and honest K-factor measurement. Never estimate K above 1 or fabricate viral results without `inputs/`.

## 1. Sneezers and IdeaVirus (Godin)

From Seth Godin (*Unleashing the IdeaVirus*, *Purple Cow*):

- **Design for the Sneezers first, not the mass market:** a viral loop fails when built for average lukewarm users. Identify the small subset in `customer.md` or referral logs who already talk, write reviews, or forward gifts repeatedly (*powerful/promiscuous sneezers*), and remove friction from their sharing moment.
- **Smoothness and Velocity:** how fast can a delighted buyer pass a real taste of the product to a peer? Use an honest **gift**, **shared deliverable**, or **sample artifact** built directly from `Core offer`.
- **Persistence:** make the shared artifact carry clear brand recognition cues (`05-distinctive-assets`) and a straightforward path for the recipient to try or inquire without pressure.

## 2. Earned talkability triggers (Barnum)

From P.T. Barnum's publicity lens: people share things that spark honest curiosity or show unmistakable proof right in front of them.

Apply Barnum cleanly inside product sharing mechanics:
- **Publicly observable proof:** give recipients something visual, surprising, or immediately useful when shared (a real diagnostic score, transparent benchmark output, physical gift card with real tasting notes, or live demo link).
- **Truthfulness constraint:** every curiosity hook or bold shareable hook must resolve immediately to verifiable facts in `offer.md` and `inputs/`. Never design dark-pattern forced shares, misleading referral rewards, or unapproved discounts.
