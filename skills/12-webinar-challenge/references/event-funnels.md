# Event funnels

| | Webinar | Challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 45–90 minutes | 3–5 days |
| Best for | warm, solution-aware buyers; mid- to high-ticket offers | less aware buyers; community or program offers |
| Structure | Perfect Webinar: big domino, three secrets, stack | a daily task and quick win; the offer on the last day |
| Key metric | show rate, then offer conversion | daily participation, then offer conversion |

## Show-up sequence

- confirmation (calendar link);
- a reminder 24 hours before;
- a reminder 1 hour before;
- a "we're live" message;
- the replay, with its real window.
