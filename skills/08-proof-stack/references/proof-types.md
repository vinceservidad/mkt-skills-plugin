# Proof types

| Type | Examples | Stronger when | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Results and data | "94% of client-months closed on time (2025 close log)" | a number, a period, a named source | your own data: say it's yours |
| Testimonials and reviews | an exact review with a date and a role | specific outcome, a named role, a third-party platform | edited or invented quotes; permission |
| Case study | before, intervention, after, with numbers | the client is named (with permission) and the numbers come from their data | cherry-picking: say it's one case |
| Demonstration or sample | a sample report on the prospect's own data; a live demo; a taste test | done on the buyer's case | — |
| Credentials and authority | years of experience, a qualification, a specialisation | relevant to the claim | credentials unrelated to the result |
| Guarantee | "if we miss the 5th, the month is free" | specific, and costly to the seller | a guarantee the business can't honour |
| Social proof | number of clients, peers who use it, logos, referrals | peers like the buyer | inflated counts; logos used without permission |
| Borrowed credibility | press, partners, awards | real and recent | implied endorsements |

## Softening a claim you can't prove

| Unprovable | Provable |
| --- | --- |
| "The best bookkeepers for agencies" | "Bookkeeping for creative agencies only, since 2021" |
| "Guaranteed results" | "Books closed by the 5th, or that month is free" |
| "Customers love us" | an exact review, with its source and date |

## Collecting missing proof

- Ask three recent clients for one specific result in their own words, and get written permission.
- Pull the log or data behind any number you want to use, and keep it.
- Make a demonstration or sample that a prospect can see on their own case.
