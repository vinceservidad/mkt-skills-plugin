# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Demand generation

Creating future demand among buyers who are not in market yet (education, content, brand reach), measured over months, as distinct from demand capture, which harvests people already searching. Say which one a channel does before judging it on short-term conversions.

## Omnichannel sequencing

The order in which channels reach the same buyer, for example paid social to introduce, search to capture, email to convert. Plan the sequence only after one or two core channels work; adding channels early spreads budget and learning thin.

## OOH, TV, radio, print

Offline broadcast and outdoor media. They buy reach and brand memory, not clicks, so judge them with reach, frequency, brand lift or geo tests, and only when the budget can sustain enough frequency to be noticed.
