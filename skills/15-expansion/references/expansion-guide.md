# Expansion guide

## Signals

| Signal | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Asked for the next thing | A client asks which clients to drop, or about project margins |
| Growth | Staff or revenue growth; a band change |
| Engagement | Attends every review, uses the core weekly |
| Not ready | Low engagement, recent complaint, under-using the core |

## Next step checks

Defined (what exactly), priced (by the owner), deliverable (capacity), and wanted (signals). Missing any: pilot with a few ready accounts.

## Net revenue retention

NRR = (starting monthly revenue + expansion − contraction − churn) ÷ starting monthly revenue, for the same accounts over the period.

## Account plan

Goals, health, risks, opportunities, owner, next review date.
