# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Expansion playbook (upsell / cross-sell)

Moving existing customers to a higher tier or an added offer. Applied with readiness signals, a real and priced next step, triggers after success, and net revenue retention.

## Account management (B2B)

A named owner for each key account with reviews, health signals and an account plan. Applied to grow and keep the accounts that matter most.
