## Source and evidence boundary

[Shopify: How a Product Seeding Marketing Campaign Works](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/seeding-marketing), published 2026-09-08; reviewed 2026-09-29.

Shopify describes product seeding as sending gifts without an explicit posting requirement, then observing voluntary content and developing suitable relationships. Its examples prioritize creator fit and distinguish a gift from a subsequent affiliate arrangement.

This is Shopify editorial guidance, not a controlled effectiveness study. The MKT application below is an original operational adaptation using this system's existing governance. No merchant result, benchmark, universal timing rule, or performance guarantee is imported. Verify current platform features separately before implementation.

## MKT application

Choose the objective first: learn creator/product fit, earn voluntary mentions, acquire rights-cleared assets, or assess attributable demand. Those objectives need different evidence. Do not promise a guaranteed number of posts from a no-obligation pilot.

Create a candidate ledger using actual audience/product relevance, contact permission, conflicting commitments and observed work. Leave unverified audience statistics unknown. Size the pilot using the supplied budget and real landed cost, including product, packing, delivery and any agreed fees; do not call a gift free media.

Produce a respectful outreach draft specifying the product and no posting obligation. If the user needs a scheduled deliverable, offer a separately agreed commissioned arrangement rather than silently changing the gift terms. Obtain applicable disclosure/contract review through the existing skill; do not prescribe a jurisdiction-specific legal conclusion here.

Track dispatched, delivered, voluntarily posted, permission received, qualified visits, attributable orders, refunds and costs separately. Posting does not itself grant advertising or reuse rights. Unique links and codes indicate attribution, not incremental sales. Ask `$tracking-measurement` for a credible counterfactual before a causal claim, and `$affiliate-marketing` for any subsequent commission program.

**Synthetic calculation:** twenty gifts at GBP 12 product cost plus GBP 5 fulfillment each cost GBP 340. Five voluntary posts imply GBP 68 per observed post before other costs, not an ROI or a guaranteed next-pilot rate.

**Deliverable:** candidate selection rationale, costed pilot, outreach draft, rights/disclosure checklist, observation ledger and a continue/adjust/stop decision tied to the chosen objective. Do not send gifts, messages or payments without approval.
