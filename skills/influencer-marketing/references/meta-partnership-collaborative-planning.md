## Choose the actual arrangement

| Dimension | Creator partnership | Retailer collaboration |
|---|---|---|
| Primary relationship | Brand and creator/partner | Brand and retailer |
| Source-described asset | Content associated with partner and brand identities | A retailer's shared catalog segment |
| Destination to verify | The approved ad destination; do not infer it from the handle | Retailer website/app where the relevant purchase happens |
| Critical prerequisite | Content/ad-use permission, identity authorization and substantiated claims | Correct catalog segment, partner access, valid inventory/destination and accessible outcome evidence |
| Existing owner handoff | Influencer owns partner/rights plan; creative owns concept; Meta owns configuration | Meta owns campaign/catalog configuration; actual partner owner supplies rights and evidence; measurement owns reconciliation |

Do not treat gifting, commissioning a creator post, licensing content, and permission to advertise under an identity as interchangeable. A visible organic post or a shared brand name does not prove any of those grants. Preserve current disclosure and qualified contract-review requirements from the owning skill.

Produce a compact relationship ledger: partner; intended outcome; approved assets; rights scope and term; account/catalog authority; destination; supplied data access; responsible person; unknowns; next verification. Do not output credentials or private customer identifiers.

Brief creators around truthful product evidence, the communication job, nonnegotiable claims/brand constraints and creative freedom. Select fit from observed work and supplied audience research, not a follower count or invented demographic profile. Return the requested partner brief and outreach draft within the same task; do not automatically send it.

For retailer-led purchases, name the actual business source of truth and reporting access. The brand's own checkout may not contain those orders. A catalog segment does not imply permission to inspect all retailer sales or a connected MCP data tool. Draft the smallest permissions/data request when access is missing rather than claim the campaign can already run.

To evaluate adding partnerships to existing activity, define the baseline and the specific change under test. The source's reported performance improvements are not a guaranteed gain, prescribed asset share or reason to change budget automatically. Costs include agreed fees, product/fulfillment, rights, production and media where in scope; attributed results still require measurement interpretation.

**Deliverable:** arrangement decision, rights/access ledger, partner and production brief, outcome plan, bounded test proposal and exact status. This does not add an external integration, publish an ad, draft binding legal terms or authorize new paid-media coverage.
