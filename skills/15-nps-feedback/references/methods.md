# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## NPS

Net Promoter Score (Reichheld): how likely are you to recommend us, 0 to 10; % promoters (9 to 10) minus % detractors (0 to 6). Applied at a steady interval with response rates reported and detractors contacted.

## CSAT / CES

Customer satisfaction (how satisfied were you with this) after an interaction, and customer effort score (how easy was this) after a task. Applied at the moment of the interaction, not as a general survey.
