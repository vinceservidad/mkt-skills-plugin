# Onboarding guide

## Activation

- In the customer's terms: the first result they bought (a first good cup, a first closed month, a first booked client).
- Window: how soon it should happen. Denominator: new customers in the period.
- Not activation: an email open, a login, a tutorial completed, a profile filled in. They are steps, not value.
- If value isn't directly visible, pick the closest true proxy (for example the second order placed, a support ticket not needed) and label it.

## Causes of early loss

| Cause | Sign | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fit | Customers outside the ICP leave early | 02-icp-persona, sales and ads targeting |
| Product or service | The thing doesn't work as promised | 04-product |
| Operations | Late, missing, wrong | Operations |
| Measurement | Data counts the wrong thing | 17-tracking-plan |
| Onboarding friction | People don't know what to do next | This skill |

## First week

One message or step per stall, timed to when the question arises: a sign-up question that sets the product up right, a welcome message with the one thing to do first, a guide at the moment of use.

## Measure

Activation rate and time to first value per cohort; early retention per cohort; the targeted ticket groups per 100 new customers.
