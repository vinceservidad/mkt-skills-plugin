# Guarantee types

| Type | Shape | Fits when | Risk to the seller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unconditional money-back | "30 days, any reason, full refund" | low delivery cost; a product people can judge quickly | refund abuse; set a time limit |
| Conditional | "Refund if you did X and didn't get Y" | the result depends on the buyer's effort | conditions that read as tricks; keep them few and fair |
| Service guarantee | "If we miss Z, you get W" (for example, the month free) | the fear is about delivery: speed, reliability | must be measurable and in the seller's control |
| Results-based | "Get X or we work free until you do" | the outcome is measurable and mostly in the seller's control | open-ended cost; cap it |
| Better-than-money-back | "Refund and keep the bonus", or "refund plus $X" | a high-trust offer and a strong expectation of results | cost per claim exceeds the price |
| Anti-guarantee | "All sales final, because…" | a scarce, instantly consumed or custom good | needs a strong, stated reason |

## Matching the fear

| Evidenced fear | Guarantee that answers it |
| --- | --- |
| "What if it's late or slow?" | a service guarantee on time |
| "What if it doesn't work for me?" | conditional or results-based |
| "What if I waste money on something I can't judge?" | first-order or first-month guarantee; taste or trial guarantee |
| "What if I lose control or get locked in?" | cancel anytime, with no penalty and stated plainly; data export |
| "What if the quality drops?" | replace or refund on any order |

## Costing

- **Worst case per customer** = the maximum remedy (one month's fee, a full refund, plus any bonus kept).
- **Likely cost** = claim rate × average remedy. Use past refund or miss rates; otherwise mark it `(assumption)`.
- **Worth it** when the extra sales won × margin is greater than the likely cost. If unknown, say what to measure after launch.
