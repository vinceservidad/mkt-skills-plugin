The following preflight is an original MKT application. The guides are not current permission specifications, legal advice or a guarantee of ad approval.

## Access map, not password collection

Record `asset | owner | required task | existing grant | missing task-level permission | approver | verified state` for the ad account, Page, Instagram identity, dataset, catalog and destination where relevant. Add finance or app-integration access only when the requested task genuinely requires it. A partner business ID, an asset's existence or portfolio membership does not prove the necessary grant.

Distinguish portfolio control from asset-level access and an individual from an agency partner or software identity. Prefer the smallest task-specific access supported by the current account. Do not request full portfolio control simply to report on ads. Do not claim to have connected an account from documentation or a screenshot of an invitation.

Never ask the user to send passwords, authentication cookies or access tokens in a report or repository. Request access through the supported permission flow, with the owner retaining control. Draft the precise access request when necessary; do not send it or alter permissions without authorization.

## Launch preflight

| Area | Check and next action |
|---|---|
| Business alignment | Keep outcome, objective, conversion location, performance goal, selected event and attribution distinct. Record the actual setting rather than assuming a default. |
| Asset and destination | Check truthful offer/product details, brand identity, rights, valid URLs and a functioning destination. Verify the ad's promise and required qualifications match the page. A text-only review cannot certify a rendered asset or checkout. |
| Measurement | Confirm required sources, event definitions, value/currency, consent and purchase/lead-quality reconciliation through the measurement owner. One fired tag is insufficient. |
| Creative and placement | Use the existing creative brief and mobile review. Inspect the intended crops/previews; do not equate a mockup with an approved ad. |
| Suitability | Translate brand requirements into current supported placement/inventory/block-list controls. Record expected reach/cost tradeoffs and use actual delivery evidence after launch. A selected filter is not a guarantee about every future impression. |
| Budget, timing and review | Verify authorized amount, dates, timezone, status and billing readiness. Leave review time contingency; the guides' typical 24-hour review description is not a launch SLA. |
| Authorization | Identify exactly what is approved, by whom, for which entities and limits. Keep publishing, spending, permissions and tracking approvals separate. |

For a rejected ad, retain its ID/version, exact reason and the affected asset/destination. Draft the smallest substantive correction or a supported appeal through the official flow. Do not evade review through superficial renaming, duplicate accounts, hidden destination changes or misleading claims. Approval can be revisited; it does not prove legal compliance, performance or that delivery has started.

**Return:** completed readiness ledger, genuine blockers versus non-blockers, precise access/creative/measurement handoffs, proposed launch sequence and owner approvals. Report `draft`, `approved`, `submitted`, `under review`, `delivering` or another observed state accurately. This reference installs no integration, grants no access and publishes no ads.
