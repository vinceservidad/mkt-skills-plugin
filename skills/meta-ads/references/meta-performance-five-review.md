## MKT application

Start with the exact requested outcome. Keep objective, conversion location, performance goal, selected event/dataset and attribution definition separate. A cheap click is not a qualified lead or sale by relabeling it. Use supplied configuration or request the decision-changing field; do not guess the account settings.

Build a five-row review using the source's domain names. For each, record the current observation, evidence quality, applicable constraint, proposed action or reason to keep, owning specialist, and success/stop condition.

Account simplification: identify demonstrated fragmentation without erasing necessary country, language, commercial or policy distinctions. Automation: evaluate a currently verified control against the actual goal and required constraints, not a blanket enable-everything rule. Creative diversification: route to `$creative-strategy` for strategically distinct concepts and supported proof; resizing the same asset does not establish a new concept. Data quality: use `$tracking-measurement` for source-to-event reconciliation before optimizing corrupted feedback. Results validation: separate platform credit, business outcomes and causal evidence.

Return the prioritized decision and complete requested work. An excellent CTR does not justify stopping a useful ad, and a short high-ROAS window does not authorize scaling. Existing-account expansion still belongs to `$optimization-scaling`; publishing and spend changes require explicit approval and actual tools.

**Deliverable:** populated review, objective alignment, selected interventions/non-interventions, coordinated creative or measurement output, test/stop rules, material unknowns and exact draft status. This reference does not add a live Meta integration or claim completion of a Meta course.
