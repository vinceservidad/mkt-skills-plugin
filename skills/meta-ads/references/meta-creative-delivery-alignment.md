## Source basis

## MKT application

Construct one alignment row before discussing tactics:

`business outcome | customer action | campaign objective | conversion location | performance goal | event/dataset | value/counting basis | audience controls | creative promise | destination | measurement window`

Leave missing fields unknown. The guide's Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion and Sales labels describe its edition. Verify current options and account eligibility before specifying configuration. A Traffic result cannot be relabeled a purchase; a survey purchase-intent result is not a completed transaction.

Review these decision areas using observed evidence, constraints and a keep/change/test disposition:

| Area | Question and required next output |
|---|---|
| Structure | Which splits serve distinct language, eligibility, offer, cost control or reporting needs? Propose consolidation only for evidenced redundant fragmentation, with protected distinctions recorded. |
| Audience | What must be included/excluded, and what is only a suggested audience? Show how a broad approach changes creative variety needs; a narrow approach may be justified by genuine constraints. |
| Placement | Which current eligible placements fit the objective, assets, rights and brand-safety constraints? Specify needed adaptations rather than assuming every asset fits every surface. |
| Creative signals | Are attention, response and downstream outcomes aligned with the promise? Separate engagement, negative feedback, events and business records instead of optimizing all of them as the same result. |
| Recommendation review | What exact opportunity-score or automation recommendation is visible, what assumption supports it, and which outcome/guardrail would validate it? A score is not profit or approval. |

The guide's auction graphic is an explanatory model, not a published engineering specification with accessible weights. Do not calculate a user's hidden auction score or claim to know why a particular person received an ad. Likewise, uneven creative delivery is not by itself a randomized creative experiment.

Its asset-count, placement-count and weekly-result recommendations are recorded in the source notes, not enforced as universal launch criteria. Match a proposed test to budget, evidence maturity and actual production capacity; do not spend more merely to satisfy a study-guide number or opportunity score.

The existing [Performance 5 review](meta-performance-five-review.md) remains the broader account review. Use this reference for the deeper creative-delivery handoff instead of duplicating that review. Route concept development to `$creative-strategy`, data integrity/lift design to `$tracking-measurement`, and every existing-system budget or coverage expansion to `$optimization-scaling`.

**Deliverable:** completed alignment row, evidenced conditions and non-problems, selected interventions, asset/measurement handoff, constraints, test window and approval needs. No ad, account or bid is changed by this knowledge tool.
