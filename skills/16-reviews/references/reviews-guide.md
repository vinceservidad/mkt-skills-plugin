# Reviews guide

## Platforms

Where buyers look matters more than where reviews are easy: Google for local and many services, Trustpilot and store reviews for e-commerce, app stores for apps, industry sites for B2B. Count what exists before adding a platform.

## The ask

- At the moment of felt value (after a result, a good box, a resolved ticket).
- Everyone at that moment, not only promoters, where the platform forbids gating (Google and Trustpilot have rules on selective solicitation; cite current policies).
- One-click link, short, one reminder.

## Incentives

Never for positive reviews. Many platforms forbid any incentive for reviews. In the US, the FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (2024) bans buying reviews and conditioning incentives on sentiment; check the current rule and date it, or mark *to confirm*.

## Replies

Thank positive reviews specifically. For negative ones: acknowledge, state the fix or how to reach a person, no argument, no private details. Reply to old unanswered ones too.

## Never

Fake reviews, staff reviews posed as customers, review gating where banned, buying reviews, suppressing negative ones.
