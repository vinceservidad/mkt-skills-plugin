# Keynote launch playbook (Jobs)

Combine Steve Jobs's Apple product-launch storytelling discipline with Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula (PLF) so pre-launch content, live events, and open-cart assets land with clarity and demonstration proof. This playbook never overrides `12-launch` Process step 1: without a confirmed true urgency date/limit and exact offer terms in `context/`, stop and ask the owner before building any timeline or file.

## 1. Frame the villain and state the leap clearly

Before presenting features, open Pre-launch / Launch storytelling using two rules from Jobs's launches:

- **Name the status-quo villain:** draw the specific limitation buyers hate from `context/message.md` (`## Enemy`) and `customer.md` (switching pains). Show why current alternatives leave buyers stuck. Never invent competitor flaws.
- **State the core leap in 10 words or less:** distill `Core offer` / `Big idea` into one memorable plain-English headline that contrasts *before* vs. *now* (written in italics or plain text, not quotation marks).

## 2. Rule of three and narrative contrast

Structure pre-launch videos, kickoff live sessions, and launch pages around **three clean beats** matching Walker's PLF arc:
1. **Why the old way is broken** (Opportunity / problem clarity, proven with buyer evidence).
2. **What changed and how it works** (Transformation / real mechanism, no inflated promises).
3. **How you get it now and why today** (Ownership / exact offer stack, real deadline or capacity limit from `offer.md`).

Keep comparison slides or launch messaging limited to three clear reasons to care rather than drowning buyers in a 20-row feature matrix.

## 3. Show, don't just tell (live proof rule)

Jobs treated live demonstrations as the emotional climax of a launch:
- Schedule at least one concrete **demonstration asset** during Pre-launch or Open Cart (a live walkthrough, screen recording, real sample output, or documented case breakdown built from real client work with permission).
- **Save one concrete surprise bonus or reveal ("one more thing") only if `offer.md` → Bonuses actually holds an owner-approved bonus.** Never invent a surprise bonus, price drop, or extension mid-launch.
- Keep true urgency strictly aligned with `offer.md`: a real close date, cohort cap, or price schedule confirmed before announcement.
