# Product Launch Formula (Walker)

| Phase | Length (typical) | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-pre-launch | 1–3 weeks | seed: a survey, a question, a hint; listen for objections |
| Pre-launch | 5–10 days | PLC 1 opportunity · PLC 2 transformation (teach) · PLC 3 ownership (what it's like to have it) |
| Open cart | 3–7 days | the offer, proof, FAQ, objection emails, a live session |
| Close | the last 24–48 hours | reminders and the real deadline |
| Post-launch | 1–2 weeks | onboarding, refunds handled, review |

## Urgency that is true

- a close date the owner will honour (the cart really closes);
- a cohort start;
- limited live seats;
- a bonus that really ends.

Not allowed: fake timers, reopening without notice, invented scarcity.
