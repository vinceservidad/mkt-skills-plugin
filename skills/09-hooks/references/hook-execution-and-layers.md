# Hook Execution Layers and Structured Variations

Use alongside `references/hook-library.md` to separate strategic intent from phrasing patterns when writing testable openings for ads, Reels, Shorts, and posts.

## 1. Five distinct hook layers

Keep these separate in every concept or hook table so variations isolate one change cleanly:

1. **Hook objective:** what the first moment must earn now (immediate situation recognition, credible curiosity, objection disarm, differentiation, direct offer capture).
2. **Tactic / frame:** structural opening shape (direct problem, question, contrast/mismatch, curiosity gap, reframe, identity/situation callout, sourced number/proof, confession/discovery open, how-to promise, risk reversal, real-stakes open).
3. **Psychological mechanism:** why it holds attention (recognition, surprise, pattern interrupt, unresolved gap, credibility, identity fit, real stakes) — a mechanism is a hypothesis, never proof of performance.
4. **Voice pattern (structural inspiration only):** conversational cadence matching the channel (`candid admission`, `discovery realization`, `shared frustration`, `compact observation`, `conversational question`, `progress moment`, `casual heads-up`). **Critical rule:** voice patterns are syntax containers only — never put synthesized lines in quotation marks or attribute them as customer VOC.
5. **Modality:** coordinated first visual frame (*seen*), opening spoken line (*said*), and sound-off *on-screen text* / headline so they reinforce one idea rather than contradicting or repeating robotically.

## 2. Eight-step hook execution discipline

1. Lock the **exact angle** from `08-message-doc` or `09-angle-matrix` before drafting.
2. Match the **awareness level** (`03-awareness-map`): cold unaware/problem-aware viewers see their world/problem first; solution/product-aware viewers see differentiation, proof, or mechanism; only most-aware buyers open straight on offer/guarantee unless context proves otherwise.
3. Choose 3–5 distinct frames rather than padding every category with weak filler.
4. Anchor every implied claim, comparison, number, or timeframe in `context/message.md` (`Proof stack`) and `context/offer.md` — drop implied comparisons with no proof.
5. Verify sound-off readability across mobile feeds and Reels safe areas (*to confirm* against platform specs in `11-paid-social`).
6. Pair each hook with its **immediate payoff** in the body plus an existing matching **destination**.
7. Flag any audience-attribute callouts (health, financial stress, personal traits) as *policy check: to confirm* (`20-compliance`).
8. Pick 3–5 hooks varying **only the opening** over a shared body and destination for isolated testing (`18-creative-testing` owns test sizing; business outcomes decide, never thumb-stop/hold rate alone).
