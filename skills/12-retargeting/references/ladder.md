# Retargeting ladder

| Rung | Usual message | Usual offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All visitors (recent) | proof, the story, the mechanism | the free asset or a demo |
| Engaged (key page, video viewers) | answer the top objection | a demo, sample or call |
| Leads (opted in, no purchase) | a case, proof, the guarantee | the core offer or a call |
| Cart or checkout abandoners | the last objection, the guarantee, help | complete the purchase (no invented discount) |
| Buyers | onboarding, the next step on the ladder | an upsell, a referral ask, or exclude |

## To confirm in the platform before launch

- the audience types and event windows available;
- minimum audience sizes;
- consent and cookie requirements in the user's regions;
- policies on sensitive categories and personal attributes.

Record where and when each was confirmed.
