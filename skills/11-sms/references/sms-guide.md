# SMS guide

## Consent

- A phone number collected for delivery, account security or support is not permission for marketing texts.
- US: the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and FCC rules require prior express written consent for marketing texts sent with automated systems; carriers also require business texting registration (10DLC) and their own opt-in and opt-out practices (CTIA guidelines). Other countries have their own rules (for example UK PECR, EU e-privacy rules, Canada CASL). Verify current official sources for each country and date them, or mark *to confirm*. Not legal advice.
- The opt-in disclosure says: the brand, what kind of messages, roughly how often, that message and data rates may apply, how to get help and how to stop. Unchecked by default. Not a condition of purchase.
- Send a confirmation text after opt-in. Honor STOP (and common equivalents) at once, and confirm the opt-out once.

## Message types

| Type | Examples | Consent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service | Shipment heads-up before a box ships, delivered, delay notice, skip reminder the customer asked for | Opt-in to service texts; keep them free of promotion |
| Conversational | Replies to a customer's own text | The customer started it |
| Marketing | Offers, launches, restocks, win-back | Documented marketing opt-in |

Adding a promotion to a service text can make it a marketing text. Keep them separate unless the person opted in to marketing.

## Cadence

- Service texts follow the event; no more than the event needs.
- Marketing texts: few, at reasonable local hours, never more than the disclosure says. Watch opt-outs per send; a jump means stop and review.

## Writing a text

Brand name first, one message, one action, short link only if needed, opt-out wording on the first message and as the law or carrier requires. No all-caps urgency, no false deadlines.

## Measurement

Per type: sent, delivered, opt-outs, clicks, orders, and for service texts the tickets they should reduce (for example "where is my box" tickets per 100 shipments before and after).
