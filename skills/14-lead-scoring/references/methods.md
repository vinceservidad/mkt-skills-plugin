# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Lead scoring

Giving leads points for fit (who they are) and intent (what they do) so sales works the best first. Applied with weights from the business's own outcomes, disqualifiers that override, bands with routing, and a regular check against won and lost deals.

## BANT

IBM's qualification check: Budget, Authority, Need, Timing. Applied at the first conversation as heard or unknown, not as a replacement for fit and intent scoring.
