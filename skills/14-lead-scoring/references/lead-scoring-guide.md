# Lead scoring guide

## Fit

Traits from the ICP: size, type, location, revenue band, the role that decides. Disqualifiers set *not a fit*.

## Intent

| Signal | Usually |
| --- | --- |
| Booked a call, asked for a proposal | Strong |
| Viewed pricing, asked about price | Medium to strong |
| Referral from a client or partner | Strong (check the data) |
| Downloaded a resource once | Weak |
| Opened emails | Not a signal (opens are unreliable) |

Weight from the business's own won and lost leads; with few outcomes, label weights *(assumption)*.

## BANT

Budget (fits a price band), Authority (decides or can bring who does), Need (a named pain), Timing (when). Record heard or unknown.

## Bands

| Band | Rule | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales now | Fit and strong intent | Personal follow-up within a set time |
| Nurture | Fit, weak intent | Helpful email sequence, invite to a call |
| Not a fit | Disqualifier | Polite reply, point elsewhere |

## Check

Every month or quarter: won and lost by band. If good leads sit in nurture or poor ones in sales now, change the weights.
