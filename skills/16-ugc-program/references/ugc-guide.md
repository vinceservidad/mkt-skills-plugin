# UGC guide

## Prompts

Tie the prompt to a moment customers already enjoy. Make it easy: a card in the box, a hashtag, one line in an email.

## Permission

Ask the creator before reuse (a comment or DM asking, a recorded yes), credit them, say where it will appear. Ads need explicit rights and often a written release.

## Giveaways and contests

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Sweepstakes (chance) vs contest (skill) have different rules |
| Entry | Platforms limit some mechanics (for example tagging friends or sharing to enter on some platforms); check current rules |
| Eligibility | Age, country, employees excluded |
| Purchase | No purchase necessary is often required for sweepstakes |
| Official rules | Sponsor, dates, prize value, how winners are chosen |
| Law | Varies by state and country; some require registration for large prizes; verify and flag legal review |

## Seeding

Free product with no obligation to post; if posting is expected, disclosure of the gift is required under endorsement rules.

## Measure

Posts, reach to non-followers, visits and orders from featured content or links. Likes are signals.
