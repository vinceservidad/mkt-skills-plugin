# Referral guide

## When to ask

Right after a moment of felt value: a result, a milestone, a good review, a renewal. Look at when referrals already happen.

## Reward types

| Type | Good when | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thank-you, no reward | Close relationships, professional services | Still say thank you every time |
| Fee credit | Subscriptions and retainers | Cost is margin, not list price |
| Gift or experience | Personal relationships | Keep it modest and genuine |
| Donation | Referrers uncomfortable with money | Pick causes customers care about |
| Two-sided (both get something) | Consumer products | Doubles the cost |
| Cash | Partners and high volume | Can embarrass clients; regulated in some professions |

## Cost

Value of a referred customer = monthly gross margin × expected months (use median tenure as an estimate) or first-year margin. Reward share = reward ÷ that value.

## Kit

A short description, the real offer, proof that exists, a link or intro template. Nothing the business can't stand behind.

## Tracking

Unique link or code, or a required "who referred you" field. Thank every referrer.

## Legal

Disclosure of incentivised referrals and limits on paying for referrals in some professions (accountants, lawyers, health) vary by place; verify or mark *to confirm*.
