# Upsell types

| Type | Where | Size relative to the purchase | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order bump | checkout, a checkbox (unchecked) | small, closely related | an expedited setup, a template pack |
| One-click upsell | right after purchase | larger; the next problem | an advisory add-on, a premium tier |
| Downsell | after an upsell is declined | lighter | a smaller version, a payment plan |
| Cross-sell | onboarding or account | related | a complementary product |

## Honest presentation

- Unchecked by default.
- The decline is plain ("No thanks"), never "No, I don't want to save money".
- The total price is shown before confirming.
- Easy to undo or refund, as the policy allows.
