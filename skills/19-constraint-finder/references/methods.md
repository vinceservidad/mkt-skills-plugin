# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Growth stages (validate → optimize)

Validate, repeat, optimize, expand. Applied to place the business with evidence before choosing work.

## Theory of Constraints

Goldratt's rule that a system's output is set by its tightest constraint. Applied by checking each candidate with numbers, naming one or co-limiting constraints, and exploiting it before adding elsewhere.
