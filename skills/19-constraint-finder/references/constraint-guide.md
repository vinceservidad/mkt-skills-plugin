# Constraint guide

## Stages

| Stage | Question | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validate | Does a defined buyer keep buying? | Cohort retention flattening, repeat purchase |
| Repeat | Can we bring more on purpose? | Channels that add customers at a known cost |
| Optimize | Are steps efficient? | Step rates, cost per customer, margin |
| Expand | New segments, offers, markets | Core stable and capacity spare |

## Stock and flow

Net change = new − lost. Settling level ≈ new per month ÷ monthly loss rate. If loss eats most of the inflow, more acquisition mostly refills a leaking bucket.

## Candidates

Demand, conversion, retention, capacity, cash, team time, measurement. Check each with a number from the same period.

## Co-limiting

Two constraints can bind together (for example losses and delivery delays that cause them). Say so, and name what would separate them.
