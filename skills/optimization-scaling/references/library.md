The owned scaling frameworks, playbooks, and templates. The method references linked from `SKILL.md` govern; the frameworks below are one-page summaries of them for quick orientation. When a summary and a method reference appear to differ, follow the method reference.

Use one only when its scenario applies, after the readiness gates.

- [Google Ads scaling](../../../library/playbooks/google-ads-scaling.md) — which controlled Google Ads step is justified.
- [Meta Ads scaling](../../../library/playbooks/meta-ads-scaling.md) — which controlled Meta Ads step is justified.
- [Cross-channel scaling](../../../library/playbooks/cross-channel-scaling.md) — where the next unit of budget creates the best verified business value.
- [Ecommerce scaling](../../../library/playbooks/ecommerce-scaling.md) — increasing contribution profit safely.
- [Lead generation scaling](../../../library/playbooks/lead-generation-scaling.md) — increasing qualified pipeline or realized revenue safely.
- [Creative scaling](../../../library/playbooks/creative-scaling.md) — whether creative supply can support additional verified demand.
- [De-scaling and recovery](../../../library/playbooks/de-scaling-recovery.md) — reducing exposure and verifying recovery without destroying valuable coverage.

## Templates

- [Scale readiness scorecard](../../../library/templates/scale-readiness.md) — gate-by-gate verdict record.
- [Scaling economics worksheet](../../../library/templates/scaling-economics.md) — formulas, cost basis, sensitivity.
- [Scaling hypothesis brief](../../../library/templates/scaling-hypothesis.md) and [scaling experiment brief](../../../library/templates/scaling-experiment.md) — one interpretable change and its test design.
- [Scaling change plan](../../../library/templates/scaling-change-plan.md) — exact step, exposure, guardrails, approval, rollback.
- [Scaling portfolio review](../../../library/templates/scaling-portfolio-review.md) — entity classification for reallocation.
- [Scaling decision log](../../../library/templates/scaling-decision-log.md) — decisions, proof level, and replication status.
- [De-scaling plan](../../../library/templates/de-scaling-plan.md) and [recovery verification](../../../library/templates/recovery-verification.md) — reducing exposure and confirming recovery.

A filled template is a decision record, not authorization. Live changes still require explicit approval.
