## One-pager

For the decider who wasn't on the call: the problem in buyer words, what the offer does, price model, guarantee as written, proof, next step. One page.

## Deck

Follows the call: situation, gap, solution, proof, price, next step. Few slides. No brand history.

## Proof and permission

Client names and quotes need written permission. Public reviews are quoted exactly with the source; using them in sales materials may still need the reviewer's permission on some platforms: mark *to confirm*.
