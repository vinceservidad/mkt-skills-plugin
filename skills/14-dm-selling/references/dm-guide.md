# DM guide

## Flow

1. Open: answer what they asked. Thank them for the specific thing (the post they commented on).
2. Qualify: one or two plain questions (what kind of business, how big, what's going on).
3. Invite: the next step for their stage (a resource that exists, a short call, the offer).
4. Close: a clear yes or a kind no; for non-fits, point elsewhere.

## Price

If the price model is public, give the range and what decides it, then ask a question. Hiding a public price behind a call loses trust.

## Speed

Set a reply target the owner can keep (same day, next business day) and a daily slot. A slow reply loses most of the conversation's warmth.

## Outbound

Only people who engaged, with a reason tied to that engagement. No mass automation. LinkedIn, Instagram and other platforms restrict automation and bulk messages; cite current rules with URL and date, or mark *to confirm*.

## Measure

Conversations, qualified, invited, booked, clients, median reply time.
