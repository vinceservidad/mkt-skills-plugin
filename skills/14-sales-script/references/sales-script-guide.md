# Sales script guide

## Frames

| Frame | Best for | Core move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CLOSER (Hormozi) | One-call close, clear offer | Clarify, Label, Overview, Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, Reinforce |
| Consultative / solution selling | Considered B2B buys | Diagnose deeply, fit the solution |
| Challenger (Dixon and Adamson) | Buyers who misread their problem | Teach, tailor, take control |
| Sandler | Qualifying early, removing pressure | Up-front contract, pain, budget, decision |
| Straight Line (Belfort) | Keeping long calls on track | Steer back to the line; build certainty |
| Setter–closer | High call volume | Split booking and qualifying from closing |

## Call shape (adjust to the sale)

1. Opening and agenda: time, purpose, and that either side can say no.
2. Who decides: names by role, how they decide, what they need to see.
3. Diagnosis: situation, problem, cost, what they tried, the outcome they want. The buyer talks most.
4. Playback: the gap in their words; ask if it is right.
5. Presentation: only the components that close the named gap, with proof that exists.
6. Price and terms: as written in the offer.
7. Objections: acknowledge, ask what is behind it, answer, check.
8. Close: a plain ask and a real next step.

## Never

- False urgency (a price rise that isn't real, a deadline that isn't true).
- Claims, results or client names without a source and permission.
- Pushing after a clear no; a polite exit keeps referrals and reputation.
- Closing without the person who actually signs.

## Measure

Calls held, qualified, proposals sent, closed, by source, with counts. Lost reasons logged one per deal. Compare before and after the script over equal periods.
