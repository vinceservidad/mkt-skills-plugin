# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Sales script

A written structure for a sales call (parts, questions, transitions, the close), used as a guide, not read aloud. Applied as diagnose, play back, present what closes the gap, handle the real objection, ask.

## CLOSER (Hormozi)

Clarify why they're here, Label the problem, Overview past pain, Sell the vacation (the outcome), Explain away concerns, Reinforce the decision. Applied for single-call closes, with the outcome sold in the buyer's words.

## Consultative / solution selling

The seller acts as an adviser who diagnoses the problem and fits a solution to it. Applied for considered B2B purchases where trust and fit matter more than speed.

## Challenger Sale

Dixon and Adamson: teach the buyer something new about their problem, tailor it to them, and take control of the conversation. Applied when buyers misread their problem (for example blaming revenue instead of late numbers).

## Sandler

A system that qualifies hard and early (pain, budget, decision) and lets the buyer say no without pressure. Applied to agree an up-front contract for the call and to qualify out poor fits.

## Straight Line

Belfort's method of keeping a call moving toward a decision while building certainty in the product, the seller and the company. Applied only for structure and control, never for pressure or false claims.

## Setter–closer model

One person books and qualifies calls (setter), another runs the sales call (closer). Applied only when call volume and roles justify it.

## Proposal and pricing presentation

How the price and terms are shown at the end of a call or in a written proposal. Applied as price, inclusions, guarantee and next step stated plainly, tied to the gap the buyer described.
