# Email structures

| Structure | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| PAS | promoting a fix to a known problem |
| BAB | showing the change the offer makes |
| Story → lesson → offer | newsletters and relationship emails |
| News → why it matters → call to action | announcements |
| Plain-text letter from a person | high-trust offers and re-engagement |

## Subject lines

- Specific: *Your March numbers, closed by the 5th*.
- Curious, but paid off in the body.
- No deception: no fake "Re:" or "Fwd:", and no subjects that pretend to be transactional.

## Body

- Open with the reader, not with "We're excited to announce".
- Keep paragraphs to one to three lines.
- One link or button for the call to action, repeated in the P.S.

## Compliance basics (check the law where the list lives)

Identify the sender, include a working unsubscribe link, and give a postal address where required. Send only to people who gave permission.
