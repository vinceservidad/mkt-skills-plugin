# Meta creative strategy: research to a decision-ready brief

```yaml
artifact_type: playbook
decision: translate supplied creative evidence into a brief and the finished concepts the user needs
scope: Meta-oriented research and brief development; channel settings and measurement retain their owners
owner: creative-strategy
inputs: business goal, product truth, audience evidence, current creative, available proof and production constraints
evidence_status: inferred
confidence: not-assessed
freshness: 180-days
dependencies: customer-research handoff, channel configuration, evidence and authorization boundaries
authorization: draft
rollback_or_stop: stop invented insights, unsupported claims or a brief that changes the approved goal
```

## Source basis

The supplied Meta Blueprint July 2026 guide, pages 5-8, connects audience/content observations to hypotheses and brief decisions. Pages 10-13 organize the brief into Business goal, Campaign objective, Audience selection, Measurement strategy and Creative concept, and call for shared terminology across teams. See [source identity and limits](meta-july-2026-source-notes.md).

The application below is an original MKT workflow built on that framing. It is not a quotation, a new platform control, or an experimentally verified improvement.

## MKT application

Start with the user's requested artifact and reuse relevant context already established. Do not repeat a full intake for a bounded rewrite. Ask only for a missing fact that could change the requested decision or make a claim unsafe. Customer language must come from traceable research; observed cultural trends are not customer testimony.

Build an evidence-to-choice ledger before developing the concepts:

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Observation | Exact source, period, creative ID or quoted research location; distinguish account results from broader trends |
| Interpretation | Plausible explanation, alternative explanation and confidence |
| Hypothesis | What would change, for whom, and which named outcome should respond |
| Creative choice | Message, visual, mechanic or format the evidence makes worth testing |
| Proof and constraint | Supported product claim, rights/brand limits and production capacity |
| Test and next decision | Comparison, primary outcome, guardrails and what would justify keeping or revising it |

A recent video with more views is an observation, not proof that video causes more orders. Compare matched contexts and keep platform allocation, audience mix and measurement differences visible. Use `$customer-research` for synthesis rather than inventing needs from an audience label.

Populate the guide's five brief components:

| Component | Required MKT brief content |
|---|---|
| Business goal | Supplied outcome, baseline when available, horizon, meaningful progress and constraints. Make a SMART goal specific without fabricating the target. |
| Campaign objective | Intended Meta objective and the desired customer action; `$meta-ads` separately verifies conversion location, performance goal and selected event. |
| Audience selection | Evidenced buying situation, context and required inclusions/exclusions; distinguish strategic audience from the platform targeting configuration. |
| Measurement strategy | Outcome definition, source, window and guardrails; label proxy measures and route causal claims to `$tracking-measurement`. |
| Creative concept | One central idea, reason to care, mechanic, supported proof, opening, format adaptations, CTA and destination. |

Add a short shared vocabulary only when definitions might diverge: purchase versus lead, actual order value versus attributed value, concept versus format, and the exact meaning of a result. Do not override established client definitions silently.

Then return the finished requested work. A request for three concepts receives three developed concepts, not just the five headings. Each concept should include angle, mechanic, exact hook or opening, visual sequence, message hierarchy, proof, CTA, required assets and draft status. Use the existing [creative brief](../../../library/templates/creative-brief.md) rather than create a competing master template. Delegate distinct copy/channel/measurement decisions within the same task; do not make the user start separate conversations for each skill.

**Quality check:** every substantive creative choice has a source or hypothesis label; concepts differ strategically; product claims are substantiated; the CTA matches the destination; scope fits capacity; measurement matches the goal. A clear brief supports execution but does not establish that the campaign will succeed.
