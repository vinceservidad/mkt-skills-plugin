## Source basis

The July 2026 guide describes creative assets and placements as two dimensions of diversification (page 39). Pages 40-43 discuss formats and AI-assisted variation; page 44 advises retaining strong ads while introducing fresh work; pages 27 and 31 relate broader reach to the need for relevant creative variety. [Source record](meta-july-2026-source-notes.md).

The following portfolio method is an original MKT adaptation. The guide's claims about matching, costs and creative volume are not guarantees, minimum budgets or permission to expand an account.

## MKT application

Inventory what exists before commissioning more. Tag each asset by audience situation, angle, mechanic, message, proof, format, length, creator/brand voice, placement derivative and actual result window. Identify repeated ideas and missing communication jobs, not merely empty format categories.

Build a compact diversification matrix:

`buyer situation | angle | mechanic | proof | format | opening | destination | changed dimension | required assets | outcome/guardrails`

Prioritize cells with a distinct reason to test and enough evidence/production capacity. A new background, headline synonym or crop may be a useful execution variant, but it does not automatically create a new strategic concept. Placement derivatives are still needed for usability; count them separately from concept hypotheses.

**Synthetic example:** a verified organizer with removable dividers can support a demonstration of compartment adjustment, a side-by-side comparison using measured dimensions, and a routine-based narrative about setting up a desk. These are candidates only when the required facts and shots are available. Three differently colored backgrounds behind the same claim do not supply equivalent strategic diversity. Do not add an invented productivity benefit or customer endorsement.

Match the production vessel to the job: a single image for one focused message, sequential media for a process or set of items, video where time/motion demonstrates the proposition. The source's catalog, collection, Instant Experience and interactive-format descriptions are dated options; the channel owner verifies current eligibility and compatibility. Do not fill every option or create new integration claims.

For proposed generative enhancements, maintain an approval ledger:

`original asset | allowed transformation | product/brand invariants | generated change | reviewed output | approved use`

Background or image/video expansion must not change product construction, labels, quantity, included items, evidence, or necessary qualifications. Text alternatives must not create a stronger unsupported claim. Animation does not convert an illustration into a tested demonstration. Use only tools actually available; the guide's tool list does not install an image generator or authorize Meta automation.

For fatigue, compare exposure, response, downstream result, delivery mix and tracking/offer changes. A single high frequency or low CTR is not a diagnosis. Preserve valuable ads, propose the smallest distinct refresh, and define a valid review window. Route unexplained performance shifts to `$performance-diagnostics` and audience/budget/coverage expansion to `$optimization-scaling`. Do not label routine replacement creatives as horizontal scaling.

**Deliverable:** developed creative cells, asset/rights dependencies, separate adaptation plan, candidate refresh sequence and decision rules. Where learning a single variable matters, isolate it in the test; a whole-concept comparison can identify a bundle effect without proving which component caused it. Use [creative learning agenda](../../tracking-measurement/references/meta-creative-learning-agenda.md) for that distinction.
