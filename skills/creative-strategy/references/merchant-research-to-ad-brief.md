# Merchant observations to an original ad brief

```yaml
artifact_type: playbook
decision: turn a relevant merchant observation into a supplied-evidence creative hypothesis and finished brief
scope: buyer/use context, demonstration, ordering explanations and operational handoffs; no transferred merchant results
owner: creative-strategy
inputs: product truth, buyer and end-user evidence, purchase objections, destination, permitted assets, operational constraints
evidence_status: inferred
confidence: not-assessed
freshness: 180-days
dependencies: customer-research handoff, approved offer, channel fit, measurement and fulfillment readiness
authorization: draft
rollback_or_stop: reject fabricated customer evidence and stop promises outside supplied product or operational scope
```

## Use a case to choose a question

Merchant interviews can suggest a useful question; they do not establish this client's customer truth or a causal mechanism. Keep the case date, what the merchant actually reported, its unresolved measurement and the proposed transfer condition visible. A forecast, several businesses' combined revenue or a headline's attribution must not become an achieved result for one ad or for MKT Skills.

| Decision worth investigating | How to develop the requested work | Evidence needed before the claim |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer and user have different concerns | Draft separate candidate angles for the evidenced purchase situation, then choose a coherent promise per piece | Actual interviews or purchase/support evidence; a parent/gift buyer is not automatically the product's end user |
| The product advantage is hard to understand online | Specify a truthful demo, comparison or sample experience that makes the relevant difference observable | Real product/asset facts, fair comparison, permitted reuse and sample costs; no fabricated sensory or efficacy result |
| Customers misunderstand how the product or order works | Carry the mechanism, variant/compatibility choice and next step from ad through page, instructions and follow-up | Actual selection, ordering, use and fulfillment process; do not promise frictionless use or hidden recurring commitments |
| A new market needs different wording | Adapt copy, overlays, destination and follow-up as one local-meaning review | Local terminology, product compatibility, currency, price basis, availability, delivery and return terms |
| An idea performed with a familiar audience | Define what a new audience has not yet seen and build a self-contained explanation/proof treatment | The actual audience and exposure context; warm-audience response is not cold-acquisition proof |
| Several people produce, approve and publish assets | Name the brief owner, approver, required source facts, rights and definition of ready before handing over files | Actual team capacity and authority; a content calendar is not an active publishing system |

Return the requested concepts, scripts, copy or production brief, with the relevant buyer question, angle, mechanic, proof treatment, CTA, destination, necessary assets, operational constraints and test question. For a short rewrite, use the relevant insight without recreating the whole strategy.

Evaluate the next decision with the real commercial outcome and conversion lag. A cheap click, share, tool prediction, attributed sale or automatic creative rotation is not a controlled test of the mechanism. Sample offers, free courses, discounts and subscriptions have costs and obligations; coordinate with the offer/economics/lifecycle owners before recommending them. A source story does not authorize publishing, gifting, contacting a customer or changing spend.

## Source cases and limits

Read on 2026-09-30. These are historical merchant observations, not transferable performance promises:

- [Youth Sports Nutrition](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/youth-sport-nutrition-conversion): distinguish the purchaser's reassurance and convenience questions from the end user's preferences.
- [CROSSNET](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/crossnet-pr): distinguish pre-purchase demonstration from the explanation needed for successful use after purchase.
- [ColorIt](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/120646021-colorit-amazon-validate-product): make a tactile differentiator understandable and separate a sample/cohort path from immediate purchase. Its revenue framing includes a forecast and business-scope conflict.
- [Revival Jewelry](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/revivial-jewlery-crowdsource): explain an unfamiliar mechanism and actual ordering steps together.
- [Ballsy](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/ballsy-subscription): gifting can involve a different buyer; a default subscription can create misunderstanding rather than durable retention.
- [KeySmart](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/keysmart-translating-ads): local meaning extends beyond translating an ad's headline.
- [Phone Loops](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/phone-loops-product-management): make production dependencies and approvals explicit.
- [Kettle & Fire](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/kettle-and-fire-choosing-channels): assess demand, contribution economics and audience context before transferring an execution to another channel.
