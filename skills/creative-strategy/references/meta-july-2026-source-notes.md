## Source inconsistencies are visible, not silently repaired

- **Page 60:** the learning-agenda table pairs Action plan with a geography-lift tool statement and Marketing mix modeling with applying learnings. This mismatch is visible in the rendered page, not just extracted text. The surrounding narrative supports questions, methods and application of findings; it does not identify the unnamed tool. MKT uses its existing experiment-learning contract for an actionable plan and does not claim the guide defines an action plan as a geography tool or MMM as a next-action list.
- **Page 67 versus pages 92 and 99:** the Brand Lift paragraph calls the ad-eligible group a control group, while the glossary describes control as unexposed and test as exposed. State this discrepancy in a source-specific explanation. For an operational test, define each arm explicitly by assigned exposure and role using MKT's existing measurement contract; do not copy an ambiguous group label into a calculation.

MKT preserves the source distinction among social, brand and business measures, but its existing economics and scaling rules still require a defined revenue/cost basis, validity, uncertainty, capacity and authorization. Attributed ROAS is not contribution profit or proof of incremental effect.
