## Source and evidence boundary

[Shopify: 9 Types of Video Marketing for Ecommerce Businesses](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/types-of-video-marketing), published 2026-09-22; reviewed 2026-09-29.

Shopify distinguishes demonstrations, comparisons, customer-created videos, behind-the-scenes material and brand stories. It relates these formats to different communication jobs rather than treating all video as interchangeable. It also advises evaluating length against useful content and viewer response.

This is Shopify editorial guidance, not a controlled effectiveness study. The MKT application below is an original operational adaptation using this system's existing governance. No merchant result, benchmark, universal timing rule, or performance guarantee is imported. Verify current platform features separately before implementation.

## MKT application

Start with the supplied question or objection, not a demand to fill every video category. A demo is a candidate when the product must be understood in use; a comparison requires verified like-for-like criteria. Missing genuine customer testimony rules out a testimonial claim, not all useful concepts.

Build a proof ledger before scripting: proposed claim, supplied evidence, what the shot can actually demonstrate, qualification, and remaining gap. A scene illustrating a hypothesis must not look like evidence of a tested result. Do not invent customer psychology to justify the format.

Produce the requested number of developed concepts when the brief supports them. For each, provide the angle, mechanic, opening words, shot sequence, on-screen text, evidence shown, CTA, destination, asset requirements and draft status. If copy or a storyboard is requested, return the work with the relevant specialist support, not only a list of video types. Format is the vessel; cosmetic crops are not new strategic concepts.

State the intended downstream outcome and what the test can distinguish. Hold major competing variables steady when attributing a difference to the creative hypothesis; audience retention is diagnostic, not proof of sales or contribution. Ask the channel owner to verify placement requirements and `$tracking-measurement` to check causal conclusions. Do not turn one successful execution into a universal angle rule.

**Deliverable:** completed concept/storyboard, proof ledger, production dependencies, test variable, primary outcome, guardrails and next decision. For a bounded rewrite, return the revision without restarting the whole strategy.
