# Ad references, endorsements and assembled copy

```yaml
artifact_type: playbook
decision: turn reference material and product evidence into original, compatible paid assets
scope: public-ad analysis, endorsements and modular/catalog copy; channel setup and test validity retain their owners
owner: creative-strategy
inputs: source material, capture date, product/offer facts, permitted uses, destination, module and product sets
evidence_status: inferred
confidence: not-assessed
freshness: 180-days
dependencies: customer-research provenance, influencer-marketing rights, channel constraints, tracking-measurement validity
authorization: draft
rollback_or_stop: stop copied execution, unsupported performance labels or assemblies that lose required conditions
```

## Public ads are observable executions

Record the source URL, capture date, advertiser, market/filter, visible format, wording/claim, offer, proof treatment, CTA and destination. Separate what was actually visible from interpretation and unavailable data. Duration, repetition, many variations, an article's praise or a brand's revenue do not reveal the ad's spend, profit, conversion rate or incremental effect. Estimated competitor metrics need their provenance and estimate label.

Explain the communication job a reference could perform, then produce an original adaptation from the user's own product truth and customer evidence. Do not copy distinctive wording, composition or trade dress. A useful analysis delivers the requested concepts or copy, with hypotheses and evidence gaps; it does not stop at warning that performance is unknown. Reviews of another brand are not quotations from this client's customers.

## Make endorsement use reviewable

For each proposed customer or creator statement, retain its exact traceable source, speaker, relevant product/use context, publication/capture date, permission scope, allowed channels/duration/edits, material relationship or incentive, required disclosure and claim limitations. Mark a missing field as missing; public visibility, gifting or a purchase does not establish advertising reuse rights.

Do not rewrite a subjective experience into a general efficacy claim, splice words into a new meaning, or create first-person testimony from product facts. If permission or authenticity is missing, still deliver a factual brand-voice alternative when the brief supports it. Customer Research owns the evidence handoff; Influencer Marketing owns creator/relationship and usage-rights planning. Specific disclosure or regulated-claim requirements need current authoritative review.

## Build compatible modules, not a bag of claims

Separate invariants from variations: product identity, approved commercial terms, genuine proof, eligibility and required qualifications must remain accurate; hooks, format, reading order or phrasing can vary within that scope. List what may combine and what must remain together. A visually polished combination can still be false.

For a catalog/product set, check each included item and destination against every static claim. A material, certification, delivery promise, discount or guarantee for one SKU cannot automatically describe the whole set. Split the set, qualify the claim, use verified item-specific content, or remove the incompatible claim. Missing per-item evidence is a gap, not permission to extrapolate.

Review candidate assemblies for standalone meaning, product/offer consistency, pronoun dependence, repeated messages, proof match, legibility, essential qualifiers and destination continuity. Define factual fallback content for missing variables. Do not assume every platform supports the same assembly logic; have the channel owner verify actual automation and preview behavior.

## Review learning honestly

AI preference scores, virtual buyer simulations and predicted outcomes are hypothesis inputs, not measured customer behavior or a winning ad. Adaptive delivery, organic posts on different days and many simultaneous changes do not by themselves establish a controlled A/B or multivariate experiment. Define the actual contrast, assignment, commercial outcome, lag, guardrails and next decision with Tracking Measurement. Return a bounded revision when evidence supports it; do not wait for a whole new strategy to fix a factual mismatch.
