## Source basis

Pages 47-53 of the supplied July 2026 guide cover the opening, branding, audio, text, image framing, safe zones and CTA. Page 54 provides nine assessment criteria, and page 55 applies them to a fictional example. The page 51 graphic shows different UI-obstruction areas for Reels and Stories. [Source identity and qualifications](meta-july-2026-source-notes.md).

The criteria names below follow the guide. Their operational tests and output requirements are original MKT additions. Timing, length and text figures in the guide remain recommendations for its edition, not current enforced limits.

## Inspect the asset actually supplied

Record asset ID/version, dimensions, duration if known, target placement and the evidence inspected. A script permits a script review, not claims about actual rendered legibility, captions or sound. A still frame cannot establish an entire video's pacing. Mark unavailable checks not inspected and request only the material missing evidence.

For every relevant row return `observation/location | pass, revise or not inspected | reason | exact revision | verification`.

| Guide criterion | Operational review |
|---|---|
| Brand identification | Identify where the brand/product becomes recognizable. Propose an earlier cue when necessary without substituting an obstructive logo animation. |
| Key brand message | State what a viewer can understand from the opening alone. Move the supported proposition forward rather than invent a stronger benefit. |
| Attention immediacy | Evaluate the actual opening image, contrast, movement or tension against the brief; attention tactics remain hypotheses, not guaranteed scroll-stoppers. |
| Sound-on and sound-off suitability | Check that essential meaning survives muted playback, and that licensed audio/voiceover adds value when audible. Verify captions and overlay readability. |
| Mobile framing and safe zones | Inspect each intended derivative at realistic display size and with its current placement UI. Keep product, claims, qualifiers and CTA legible; distinguish crop survival from interface clearance. |
| Call to action | Show the exact requested next step, its timing and destination. Do not simulate a platform button that misleads about the actual action. |
| Lift study alignment | Check whether the creative can communicate the brand or purchase-intent construct the study actually measures. Route study validity to the measurement owner. |
| Complementary assets | Identify a specific message/format gap that another asset would address. Do not mandate more assets simply because one ad exists. |
| Audience relevance | Use supplied audience situation, language and product context. Do not diagnose a mismatch solely by guessing the age or identity of people pictured. |

## Revise, then verify

Return the revised opening, exact overlay/caption copy, shot order, brand/proof/CTA treatment, audio direction and derivative plan requested by the user. For a static asset, apply the relevant rows and mark time/audio-only criteria not applicable. Keep the established [static DTC direction](static-dtc-creative-direction.md) and [hook execution](hook-execution.md) contracts rather than introduce a second style system.

The guide's early 2-3 second emphasis is a review cue. Its short-video and 125/40/25-character recommendations do not justify rejecting all longer videos or truncating required product qualifications. A longer treatment may need a shorter adaptation; current technical rules are checked separately by `$meta-ads` before publication.

A centered square core can preserve meaning when deriving 1:1 from 4:5. It is not a universal Reels/Stories safe zone. The source graphic has asymmetric Reels UI areas; use an actual placement preview instead of inventing one margin for every surface. Check every requested crop independently, including text and proof, not only the bottle or hero subject.

**Output:** annotated nine-row review, prioritized revisions, completed revised brief/script/copy, preview checklist and exact draft/approved status. A clean preflight proves only the checks performed. It does not establish media eligibility, business lift or publication.
