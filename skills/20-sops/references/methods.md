# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## SOPs / playbooks

A written repeatable process with steps, owners, timing, examples and checks. Applied only to processes that have worked at least twice.

## Creative production pipeline

Brief, draft, review, final, publish, with owners and turnaround. Applied to the SOP's creative steps.

## Agency and freelancer management

Briefing, reviewing, approving and paying outside help. Applied with a brief template and named approver.

## Brand guidelines

The voice and visual standards work must meet. Applied as the minimum the SOP needs, from context or marked to define.
