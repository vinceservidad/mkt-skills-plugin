# SOP guide

## Structure

Purpose · runs it is based on · steps (owner, when, input, output, example) · checks · pipeline · brief template · brand standards · owner and review date.

## Checks

Put a check where it broke before: for example "stock loaded and test order placed before the page goes live".

## Freelancer brief

Goal · audience · message · format and size · deadline · budget · examples · who approves.

## Keep it short

One page of steps; detail in linked examples.
