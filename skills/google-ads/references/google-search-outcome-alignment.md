## MKT application

Record an alignment chain: primary business outcome -> measured conversion action -> campaign goal and action status -> supplied value basis -> bidding objective -> eligible queries -> ad promise -> landing destination. A break in that chain is a reason to investigate, not permission to replace every campaign.

Distinguish a genuine purchase or qualified lead from a supporting signup, page view or engagement. Use the existing tracking owner to validate collection, counting, values and lag. Revenue, contribution and predicted lifetime value must keep their actual definitions; do not manufacture values to justify value-based bidding.

Use the source's recommendation as a candidate, not a universal migration instruction. Protect brand/product coverage and explicit geography, policy and budget constraints. An impression-share objective or unreliable commercial signal does not inherit a blanket broad-match recommendation. Broadening an existing account routes to `$optimization-scaling` before implementation.

When testing is justified and authorized, return the exact current/proposed scope, a hypothesis, protected coverage, valid comparison, outcome and quality guardrails, lag-aware review rule and rollback condition. Do not infer success from extra clicks or an optimization score. Confirm current experiment and bidding controls through official documentation and the actual account rather than the 2023 interface description.

For a requested build, coordinate the channel structure and finished assets with `$creative-strategy` and `$copywriting` as appropriate; deliver the work supported by the brief. State draft status and any decision-changing gaps. The source's commercial advice does not grant account access or permission to publish.
