# Hypothesis guide

## Template

Because we see [evidence, source], we believe [change] for [audience] will move [metric] by [expected size, assumption]. We'll know when [measure] after [duration].

## Scoring

| Framework | Parts | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICE | Impact, Confidence, Ease (1 to 10) | Fast triage |
| PIE | Potential, Importance, Ease | Page-level CRO |
| RICE | Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort | Product and larger teams |

Confidence: high only with direct evidence (tickets, research, past tests); low for opinion.

## Traffic check

If a page gets a few hundred visitors a day and converts at a few percent, small changes need months of traffic to detect. Prefer bigger changes, higher-traffic steps, or non-A/B methods.

## Minimum viable tests

Fake door, manual concierge, survey, prototype, one-page variant, before-after with caution.
