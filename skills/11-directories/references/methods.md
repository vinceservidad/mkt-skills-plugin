# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Directory submissions

Listing the business on directories, review sites, marketplaces and partner listings. Applied as a fit test per place (does the buyer look there), a consistent honest profile, real reviews, and each listing judged on the leads it sends.

## Product Hunt / launch platforms

Launch communities where makers post new products for a day of attention. Applied only when the platform's audience is the buyer; planned without vote buying, and judged on customers rather than upvotes.
