# Directories guide

## Types

| Type | Good for | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local business profile (maps) | Local search, reviews, address and hours | Name, address and phone must match the website exactly |
| Industry or trade directory | Buyers comparing vendors in one industry | Check that buyers use it; many trade directories get little traffic |
| Review and comparison sites | Buyers comparing at the decision step | Need real reviews; paid tiers are owner decisions |
| Software partner or advisor directories | Buyers who already use that software | Membership or certification must be real and current |
| Marketplaces | Buyers who shop there | Fees, rules and the marketplace owning the customer relationship |
| Launch platforms (Product Hunt and others) | Products for makers, tech workers and early adopters | Little value for non-tech buyers; rules ban buying or soliciting votes |
| Generic web directories and link farms | Rarely anything | Can look spammy and are not worth the time |

## Fit test

1. Evidence the ICP looks there: discovery calls, sales notes, interviews, competitors' presence, the owner's own buyers saying so.
2. Something the listing can show that helps the buyer decide (reviews, proof, price model, certification).
3. Cost and time to keep it current.
4. A way to count leads from it (source field, unique link).

Fail on 1 means no.

## Launch platforms

Before any launch: who votes and comments there, and are they the buyer? Another product's upvotes are not evidence for this business. If it fits: pick a date, prepare the listing, a short maker comment, real screenshots, and someone to answer comments all day. Never buy upvotes or run vote rings. Cite the platform's current rules with URL and date.

## Reviews

Ask every customer at a good moment, not only the happy ones where a platform forbids review gating. Never pay for or write reviews. Reply to all. Rules differ (for example platform policies and, in the US, FTC rules on fake reviews and testimonials); cite or mark *to confirm*.
