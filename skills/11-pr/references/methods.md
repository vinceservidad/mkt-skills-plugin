# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## PR / earned media

Coverage the business does not pay for: articles, interviews, podcast spots, mentions. Applied as a story inventory with sources and permissions, outlets chosen on buyer fit, one pitch per outlet, and coverage judged on the conversations it starts.

## Newsjacking

Adding a useful, true angle to a news story while journalists are still writing about it (David Meerman Scott). Applied only to real, dated news where the business has something to add, never to a tragedy, and never by inventing a hook.
