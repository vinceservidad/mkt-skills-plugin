# Earned media classics (Bernays & Barnum)

Use Edward Bernays's engineering-of-consent methods and P.T. Barnum's publicity mechanics inside strict truth and permission boundaries. Never invent studies, stage fake crowds or events, or mislead journalists.

## 1. News-event PR and third-party authority (Bernays)

From Edward Bernays (*Crystallizing Public Opinion*, *Propaganda*): journalists ignore company announcements (*"We launched v2"*) because announcements are self-interest. They cover **created news events** that reveal an independent truth or affect the public/industry.

### How to engineer real news stories cleanly

1. **Create an objective, verifiable artifact:** run an honest original survey, analyze anonymized delivery/benchmark data (only when customer contracts and owner permit it via `11-pr`), release an open-source checklist/tool, or host a live public benchmark or roundtable.
2. **Map third-party authorities:** list respected peers, trade associations, researchers, or practitioners whose readers match `ICP and personas`. Do not pitch buyers straight; pitch the trusted voices and outlets those buyers already read.
3. **Frame around the reader's problem, not your SKU:** open the pitch with the real trend, customer cost, or industry question backed by dated facts in `inputs/`. Mention your business cleanly as the source/operator behind the findings.

## 2. Earned publicity showmanship (Barnum)

From P.T. Barnum's publicity principles: a dry truth gets ignored if nothing invites curiosity or public conversation. Showmanship earns attention when you turn a provable product capability into a visible, talk-worthy event or demonstration.

### Permitted Barnum mechanics (strict truth rule)

- **Live public demonstrations:** instead of writing *"our process works fast"*, invite prospects or reporters to watch a live teardown, recorded speed test, or open comparison using real, disclosed inputs.
- **Bold public challenges:** offer a transparent, risk-free test backed by an actual guarantee in `offer.md` (*for example, an open challenge backed by verifiable criteria*).
- **Honest curiosity hooks:** give an event, report, or comparison an intriguing title anchored directly to the real finding.

Strict Barnum guardrail: Barnum worked in an era of carnival humbug; modern PR destroys brand trust the moment a claim or stunt stretches facts. Every demonstration, stunt, or challenge in `11-pr` MUST prove a real, verifiable capability in `context/offer.md` and pass `claims and sources` verification with zero fabrication.
