# Price testing guide

## Designs

| Design | Fairness | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sequential cohorts (price A for new buyers in period 1, B in period 2) | High | Watch seasonality; use matched periods |
| Regions | Medium to high | Regions must be comparable |
| Offer variants (bundle, term, tier) | High | Tests packaging as well as price |
| Simultaneous split for the identical item | Low | Can break trust and may raise legal issues; disclose, honor lower price on request |
| Survey (Van Westendorp, Gabor-Granger) | High | Stated preference, not behaviour |

## Metrics

Revenue per visitor, contribution margin per visitor, conversion, retention and refunds over the decision window.

## Existing customers

Keep their price unless the owner decides a change with clear notice.

## Legal

Consumer-protection and pricing rules (for example on reference prices, discriminatory pricing, auto-renewal changes) vary; verify or mark *to confirm*.
