# Prospecting guide

## Sizing

Accounts needed = new clients wanted ÷ (reply-to-meeting rate × meeting-to-client rate) ÷ reply rate, using the business's own rates where they exist. If a rate is unknown, write the formula, mark the input *(assumption)* and update it after the first batch. Never above capacity.

## Sources

| Source | Good for | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referrals and introductions | Highest trust | Ask clients and partners; see 16-referral-program |
| Company websites and public pages | Size, type, signals | Record source and date |
| Public directories and associations | Lists by industry | Check terms of use |
| Events | Attendees who fit | Use lists only as attendees agreed |
| Bought or scraped personal data | Never | Consent, legal and reputation risk |

## Signals

A hire, a new office, funding, a public post about the problem, a leadership change. Keep the date and the source. Stale or unrelated signals are not reasons.

## Cadence

Several touches over two to four weeks, mixing channels the buyer uses, each with something new. Stop at reply, a no or an opt-out.

## Measure

By source and tier: contacted, replies, meetings, qualified, proposals, clients.
