# Editing checklist

## 4Cs (Bly)

- **Clear:** one idea per sentence; no jargon the reader doesn't use; short paragraphs.
- **Concise:** cut "very", "really", "in order to", hedges ("we believe"), and repeated points.
- **Compelling:** lead with the reader's outcome; "you" over "we"; active verbs.
- **Credible:** a number, a name or role, a date; proof next to the claim.

## Techniques

- **Slippery slide (Sugarman):** every sentence earns the next; the first sentence is short.
- **Bucket brigades:** short transitions such as *Here's the thing:* (sparingly).
- **Damaging admission:** an honest limitation raises credibility. Keep the real ones.
- **Future pacing:** let the reader picture the after-state, without promising beyond the offer.

## Claims

| Copy says | Ask | Edit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The best…" | proof? | replace with a specific, provable difference, or flag it |
| "Guaranteed results" | is it in offer.md? | use the exact guarantee terms, or flag it |
| "Thousands of happy customers" | the real number? | use the sourced number, or flag *needs proof* |
