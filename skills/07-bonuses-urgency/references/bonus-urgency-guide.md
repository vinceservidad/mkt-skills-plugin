# Bonuses and urgency guide

## Bonus patterns by objection

| Objection | Bonus that removes it |
| --- | --- |
| "It's a lot of work to start" | done-for-you setup or migration; a document chase; a quick-start session |
| "Will it work for someone like me?" | a sample or audit on their own data; a case study from a peer |
| "I won't know how to use it" | a guide, a short course, a checklist, a recorded walkthrough |
| "I'll fall off after the start" | check-ins, an accountability call, a progress report |
| "It's not the right size for me" | a lighter starter option (pass this to `07-value-ladder`) |
| "My team won't adopt it" | team onboarding, templates, an internal pitch kit |

## Valuing a bonus honestly

- **Allowed:** "Worth it to the buyer because it saves about X hours of their own time." The reasoning is stated, and inferred numbers are marked `(assumption)`.
- **Allowed:** what the buyer would pay elsewhere for the same thing, if there is evidence.
- **Not allowed:** an invented "valued at $997" with no basis.

## True scarcity and urgency

| Type | True when | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capacity | delivery is limited by people or stock | "We onboard 4 new agencies a month" (if true) |
| Cohort or deadline | a start date or a price change is already decided | a cohort starts on the 1st; a price rises on a set date |
| Seasonal | the buyer's own calendar creates a deadline | "Start by the 20th to have next month closed by the 5th" |
| Cost of waiting | each month of delay costs the buyer something real | another month of decisions made without numbers |

**Not allowed:**
- countdown timers that reset;
- "only 3 left" when stock is unlimited;
- "today only" offers that return tomorrow;
- invented demand ("200 people are looking at this").

These break trust and, in many jurisdictions, consumer protection law.
