# Lead magnet formats

| Format | Quick win | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checklist | do X in order | process problems |
| Template or spreadsheet | fill in and use today | planning, forecasting |
| Calculator | a number about their situation | cost-of-problem awareness |
| Quiz or assessment | a result about them | segmentation, unaware traffic |
| Sample on their data | see the product on their case | solution-aware, B2B |
| Mini-course (3–5 short lessons) | learn one skill | education-led offers |
| Guide | understand one decision | problem-aware traffic |

## Hormozi's tests

- **Specific:** solves one narrow problem for one buyer.
- **Fast:** a win in minutes.
- **Valuable:** they would pay for it.
- **Leads to the offer:** solving it reveals the next problem, which the core offer solves.
