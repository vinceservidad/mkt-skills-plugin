# Creative Mechanics, Diversification and Iterating from Winners

Use after an angle is chosen (`09-angle-matrix` or `08-message-doc`) to design structurally distinct creative concepts and disciplined post-test iterations.

## 1. Layer boundaries (never blur these terms)

- **Angle:** strategic reason the buyer cares at their awareness level.
- **Creative mechanic:** communication engine making the audience feel, infer, or verify that angle (travels across formats).
- **Concept:** specific scene, narrative, comparison, or idea carrying the angle + mechanic + proof + offer.
- **Hook:** opening expression in the first seconds or lines (`09-hooks`).
- **Visual format / adaptation:** production vessel (UGC, static card, carousel, demo reel) and placement crops (`4:5`, `9:16`, `1:1`). Resizing or background color swaps are adaptations, not new concepts.

## 2. Nine creative mechanic families (choose by communication job, not quota)

1. **Direct demonstration:** product/mechanism shown performing its real job without staged outcomes.
2. **Controlled contrast:** fair side-by-side juxtaposition vs familiar alternative with comparable baselines.
3. **Reframe:** challenges an evidenced buyer assumption with a truer interpretation.
4. **Third-party witness:** genuine customer/creator/expert proof with verified source and permission.
5. **Implied conclusion:** observable proof/juxtaposition leads viewers to the conclusion truthfully.
6. **Discovery or conversation:** comment/question/DM discovery context (never fabricate simulated buyer screenshots as real evidence).
7. **Narrative reveal:** tension-building sequence earning the product/mechanism reveal cleanly.
8. **Proof stack:** compatible independent evidence layers (reviews + product specs + guarantee).
9. **Educational resolution:** teaches a genuinely useful process/distinction first, then bridges naturally to the offer.

## 3. Creative diversification matrix & AI invariant ledger

Build a diversification table tracking:
`buyer situation | angle | primary mechanic | proof + permission | format | opening hook | destination | changed variable | test metric & guardrail`

When proposing generative or platform AI creative enhancements (e.g., background expansion, animation, cropping), maintain an invariant rule: **never alter product construction, labels, ingredients, counts, included items, legal disclaimers, or imply unverified live performance**. Do not treat platform diversity scores or high creative counts as proof of return, and never force spend expansion outside `19-scale-paid`.

## 4. Iterating from a winner (single-variable discipline)

1. Confirm the win against the pre-set **business outcome** (orders, trials, qualified calls, unit margin) and sample threshold — never CTR/thumb-stop alone.
2. Identify what that winning cell actually varied relative to the losing comparison cells.
3. **Hold the winning angle fixed** and change **exactly one** variable next round: same script with a new hook (`09-hooks`), same hook with a new presenter, same angle with a stronger proof element, or a new format adapting the winning message (`18-creative-testing` designs the test).
4. Require replication in a comparable follow-up test before treating an angle as durable; cross-platform scaling to new channels or budgets hands off to `11-paid-social` / `19-scale-paid`.
