# Demand Gen & YouTube Creative Playbook (Variety, ABCD, Partnership Ads & Fatigue)

Distilled from official `@GoogleAds` creative playbooks (*Demand Gen campaign creative, explained: asset variety, testing, and fatigue*, *YouTube ABCDs*, and *Asset Studio*). Creative specs, aspect ratios and feature availability change — verify current official Google Ads Help documentation (cite URL + date) or mark *to confirm*.

## 1. Format & Asset Variety Across Surfaces

Demand Gen and YouTube span very different viewing postures (lean-back horizontal TV/desktop In-Stream, rapid-swipe vertical mobile Shorts, visual feed cards on Discover and Gmail). Recycling one landscape commercial across every slot destroys attention.
- **Cover all three aspect ratios deliberately:**
  - **Vertical `9:16` (Shorts-first):** Full-screen mobile framing, keep key text and product inside Shorts safe zones (avoid bottom caption/CTA and right-side engagement buttons), instant visual hook in the first 2 seconds.
  - **Horizontal `16:9` (YouTube In-Stream / Connected TV):** Traditional wide framing built for the 5-second skip clock.
  - **Square `1:1` and Landscape `1.91:1` stills / carousels (Discover & Gmail):** Crisp visual hierarchy readable on small feed cards with zero cluttered text walls.
- **Creative diversity minimum:** Pair genuinely distinct angles and visual styles (not just color swaps) — plan multiple distinct videos and multiple distinct static images across formats per ad group (verify current Google Ads recommended asset counts in official docs).
- **Isolate when native formats conflict:** If Shorts-native vertical creator videos behave very differently from Discover/Gmail image cards, use Demand Gen **Ad-Group Channel Controls** (`11-paid-search`) to isolate vertical assets to YouTube Shorts rather than diluting pacing.

## 2. YouTube ABCD Structure (Built for the 5-Second Skip Clock)

Apply Google's evidence-backed **ABCD** structure to every video concept:
- **Attract:** Open immediately on movement, contrast, tight framing or the exact buyer pain in the first 2–5 seconds (no slow logo fades or throat-clearing intros).
- **Brand:** Introduce the product/brand naturally early (first 5 seconds) and reinforce visually and verbally so viewers remember who solved the problem.
- **Connect:** Show real people, real product use and concrete proof from `context/message.md` (never fabricated claims or unapproved customer quotes).
- **Direct:** Close with one clear spoken AND on-screen call to action matching the landing page offer and traffic temperature.
- Always design for **both sound-on AND sound-off** (clear visual storytelling + burnt-in/clean captions).

## 3. Creator Partnership Ads & Asset Studio Guardrails

- **Partnership Ads (creator-led vertical video):** Native creator explanations, demonstrations and reviews (with explicit creator/usage permission — `09-ugc-brief`, `11-influencer`) often outperform polished corporate commercials on Shorts and Discover because they match the feed's native trust.
- **AI Asset Studio / generative resizing:** Use AI tools to speed up aspect-ratio adaptation or storyboard iteration, **never** to invent unverified product claims, fake before/after visuals, or off-brand typography. Every generated cut still passes brand-asset and truth checks before launch.

## 4. Spotting & Refreshing Creative Fatigue

- Track concept performance on **cost per business outcome** alongside frequency, view/thumbstop rate and CTR trends over time.
- Treat rising frequency + falling CTR/conversion rate as a **fatigue hypothesis** — check whether audience saturation or offer/landing-page issues changed before retiring a winner.
- Refresh winners systematically: keep the proven winning angle and swap strictly **one** variable at a time (new opening 3-second hook, new visual setting, new proof beat) rather than discarding the angle altogether (`18-creative-testing`).
