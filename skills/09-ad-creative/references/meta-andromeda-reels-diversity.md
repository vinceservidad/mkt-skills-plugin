# Meta Andromeda, Creative Diversity, Reels Essentials and Advantage+ Creative Guardrails

Use when designing Meta ad concept sets, auditing stagnant or repetitive ad accounts, scripting vertical Reels ads, or reviewing Advantage+ Creative enhancements. Treat retrieval, similarity, and Reels specs as platform mechanics to confirm against current Meta documentation and account previews.

## 1. Meta Andromeda and creative diversity blueprint

Modern retrieval-led ad delivery groups near-identical creative (color swaps, caption tweaks, first-word edits) together instead of treating them as separate bets. Give the system genuinely distinct concepts across five dimensions:

| Dimension | What changes between concepts | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persona | Who the ad speaks to (e.g., busy parent vs. specialty hobbyist) | Unlocks non-overlapping buyer pockets |
| Desire or pain | Core job, fear, or enemy (`09-angle-matrix`) | Different buyers act on different triggers |
| Awareness stage | Unaware story vs. problem agitation vs. solution demo vs. offer | Prevents mismatching cold vs. warm traffic |
| Creative mechanic | Demo, contrast, reframe, third-party witness, founder reveal, proof stack | Avoids visual and structural similarity |
| Format and fidelity | Static card, carousel, lo-fi creator UGC, high-contrast studio demo | Feeds distinct placement inventories cleanly |

Resizing `4:5` to `9:16` or changing background hex values is an adaptation, never a new concept.

## 2. Creative similarity vs. creative fatigue triage

Separate three distinct delivery failures before throwing out a campaign:
- **Creative similarity / limited variety:** Ads inside the same ad set share the same visual structure, angle, and opening hook, fighting over one narrow pocket of buyers. Fix by introducing new angles, mechanics, or formats (`09-angle-matrix`, `09-ad-creative`).
- **Creative fatigue:** Frequency rises on a previously winning concept while CTR and cost per business outcome worsen at stable targeting and budget. Fix by producing fresh executions around the winning angle or testing a fresh hook (`09-hooks`). Frequency alone never proves fatigue; rule out seasonality, offer changes, or tracking defects first.
- **Audience saturation or margin cap:** Rising CPM and falling marginal new customers occur across fresh concepts too. Hand budget, placement, or geo expansion decisions to `19-scale-paid`.

## 3. Meta Reels creative essentials (*to confirm* against current Meta Ads Guide)

1. Build native `9:16` vertical first rather than letterboxing horizontal spots, keeping critical visuals, captions, and offers inside current Reels/Stories safe zones (`11-paid-social` checks Ads Manager preview).
2. Win the first 3 seconds simultaneously with **seen** (visual movement or contrast), **said** (spoken hook), and **on-screen text** so sound-off and sound-on viewers catch the premise immediately (`09-hooks`).
3. Maintain visual momentum with purposeful cuts, overlays, or step-by-step beats tied to proof, avoiding slow logo bumpers or static talking heads without visual progression.
4. Test lo-fi organic creator delivery against studio product demonstrations as competing hypotheses rather than assuming one always outperforms (`09-ugc-brief`).
5. End with an explicit spoken, visual, and on-screen call to action matching the exact destination and terms in `context/offer.md`.

## 4. Advantage+ Creative enhancements guardrails (*to confirm* in Ads Manager)

Inspect every automated creative toggle individually at the ad level rather than accepting account defaults blindly:
- **Safe to evaluate per concept:** Minor brightness/contrast adjustments or standard audio-track additions where brand mood fits, verified in placement previews before launch.
- **Keep off unless explicitly approved and checked:** Dynamic text overlays, automated music over voiceover, image expansion that invents background context, or catalog cropping that cuts off product count, ingredients, price, guarantee text, or legal disclaimers.
- **Invariant rule:** Never allow platform AI or enhancement toggles to alter product construction, label text, claims, prices, or compliance disclosures.
