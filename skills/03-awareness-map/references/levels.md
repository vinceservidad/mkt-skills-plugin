# The five levels of awareness (Schwartz)

| Level | The buyer… | Lead with | Typical proof | Typical offer or next step | Headline shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unaware | feels a symptom but does not connect it to a problem you solve | a story, an identity ("people like you"), or a surprising truth about their situation | a peer's story | free content, a quiz, a checklist | story or identity hook; no product name |
| Problem aware | knows the problem and its cost, but not that a good solution exists | the problem, sharpened and named in their words; then the cost of leaving it | the cost of the status quo, in numbers they recognise | a diagnostic, a calculator, a guide | name the problem; promise relief |
| Solution aware | knows the kinds of solution and compares them | your mechanism or difference: why this way works when the others didn't | a comparison, a demonstration, a sample | a demo, a call, a sample using their data | the mechanism or the unique benefit |
| Product aware | knows you, but isn't convinced or hasn't acted | proof and answers to the specific objections | testimonials, case studies, a guarantee | a call with pricing, a trial | proof plus the strongest claim |
| Most aware | wants it and is waiting for the right deal or moment | the offer itself: terms, price, deadline (only if true) | guarantee, terms | the deal | the offer and the reason to act now |

## Estimating the share at each level

Read what the evidence shows:
- **Questions asked on calls.** "Do you work with agencies like us?" means solution aware; "What is this?" means problem aware or less.
- **Lead sources.** Referrals are usually product aware. Search for the category is solution aware. Search for the symptom is problem aware.
- **How buyers describe the problem.** No name for it means unaware or problem aware.
- **Search terms and ad comments**, if supplied.

If there is no such evidence, give the order of levels by likely size, mark it `(assumption)`, and say what data would confirm it.

## Awareness × sophistication

When `market.md` → Market sophistication exists:
- **Early stage:** a direct claim works.
- **Middle stage:** enlarge the claim, or introduce a mechanism.
- **Late stage:** identify with the buyer rather than out-claim competitors.

Apply this to the solution-aware and product-aware entries especially.
