# Landing page by temperature

| Temperature | Offer | Sections | Form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold | free asset | headline (problem or outcome), what they get, a quick proof point, opt-in | email only, perhaps a name |
| Warm | demo, sample, trial | headline (mechanism or proof), how it works, proof, objections, call to action | the fields the call or demo needs |
| Hot | the offer | headline (offer), terms, guarantee, proof, FAQ, buy or book | the checkout or booking fields |

## Message match

The headline repeats the promise of the ad, using its words and its offer. Mismatch is the most common reason clicks don't convert.

## Attention ratio

One goal per page. Remove the navigation and links that compete with the call to action.
