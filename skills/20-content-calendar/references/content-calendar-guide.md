# Content calendar guide

## Work back

Campaign date → send or post dates → final assets → drafts → brief. Add lead time for freelancers.

## Capacity

Hours per week ÷ hours per item = items per week, minus a buffer.

## Item fields

Date · channel · format · job or journey stage · owner · draft due · status.

## Seasonal

Gift seasons, Black Friday and Cyber Monday, New Year routines; check exact dates each year.
