## Source and evidence boundary

[Shopify: How To Create an SEO Road Map (With Template)](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/seo-roadmap), published 2026-09-02; reviewed 2026-09-29.

Shopify presents a roadmap as an execution plan connecting goals, tasks, resources, ownership and success measures. It recommends prioritizing dependencies, impact and feasibility, then reviewing the plan as evidence or resources change.

This is Shopify editorial guidance, not a controlled effectiveness study. The MKT application below is an original operational adaptation using this system's existing governance. No merchant result, benchmark, universal timing rule, or performance guarantee is imported. Verify current platform features separately before implementation.

## MKT application

Translate each material finding into a ticket the actual implementer can use. Record the exact URL/template or system scope, observation and source, business relevance, proposed change, dependency, acceptance evidence, owner, supplied effort estimate, risk and review trigger. Do not invent crawl results, demand, engineering estimates or an exact traffic gain to complete fields.

Use dependencies rather than a cosmetic priority score. When evidence shows valuable pages cannot be indexed, resolving that blocker can precede refinements whose value depends on indexing. Do not use this as permission to change canonicals, remove indexed pages or redirect valuable URLs without inspecting their role and obtaining approval.

Fit the work to the stated budget and delivery capacity. Separate ready work from blocked work and future candidates; avoid filling an arbitrary 90-day schedule. An unknown effort estimate is a planning question, not a reason to fabricate one. Existing page performance and query intent determine whether to improve, consolidate or create content.

Measure delivery acceptance separately from marketing outcome. A fix deployed successfully does not prove qualified traffic or revenue increased because of it. Record baseline, agreed observation window and competing changes; use the existing measurement owner for causal conclusions. Rankings and impressions remain supporting signals, not a substitute for the business objective.

**Deliverable:** prioritized and dependency-linked backlog, explicit non-priorities, production/copy briefs where requested, resource gaps, verification steps and review decisions. The source's illustrative timelines are not client promises, and a Shopify article does not establish a new runtime tool or platform entitlement.
