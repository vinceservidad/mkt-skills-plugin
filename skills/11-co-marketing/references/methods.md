# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Co-marketing / partnerships

Two businesses with the same buyer promote together: a joint webinar, shared content, a bundle, an integration. Applied as a fit test, the smallest format that tests fit, each side promoting to its own list, and leads counted per partner.

## Dream 100

Brunson's list of the places your dream customers already gather (newsletters, podcasts, communities, influencers), worked over time to get in front of their audiences. Applied as an evidence-based list of places buyers follow, ranked by fit and reachability.

## Newsletter sponsorships

Paying for a placement in someone else's newsletter. Applied as fit first, platform figures over media-kit claims, one test issue before a package, and cost per qualified lead.
