# Co-marketing guide

## Partner types

| Type | Format that usually fits | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complementary business with the same buyer | Joint webinar, co-written guide, integration listing | Overlap with your offer, their own data rules |
| Community or coach | Guest session, member resource | Who the members really are; paid-per-sale deals go to 11-affiliate-jv |
| Newsletter | Sponsored slot, guest piece, swap of mentions | Media kit is the seller's claim; ask for platform figures |
| Podcast | Guest spot | See 11-podcast-video |
| Local institution (bank, chamber, coworking) | Co-hosted breakfast or workshop | Attendance unknown until registrations |

## Data rules

- A list swap shares personal data. Only possible where every contact agreed to that sharing, which is rare. Default: no swap.
- Joint promotion: each side emails its own list about the joint event; the registration form tells registrants who will receive their details and lets them choose.
- Laws: US CAN-SPAM for email; UK and EU GDPR and PECR, Canada CASL and US state privacy laws for sharing data. Verify current official guidance and date it, or mark *to confirm*. Not legal advice.

## Sponsorship checks

1. Audience fit: who reads it (role, company size, country), from platform data, not the media kit.
2. Ask for: delivered, unique opens or clicks on recent sponsor slots, audience breakdown, and the dates.
3. Test one issue with a unique link and a clear next step (a resource that exists, a booked call).
4. Judge: cost per click, per lead, per qualified call, per customer, with counts.

## Terms

Deliverables, dates, promotion each side does, approvals, brand use, data handling, how results are shared, cost and who pays, end date and what happens after.
