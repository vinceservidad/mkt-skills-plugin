# Journey stages and intent

| See–Think–Do–Care | Buyer's journey | Usual awareness level | The buyer is… | Typical intent | Typical assets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See | Awareness | unaware, problem aware | the whole audience that could ever buy; not looking yet | informational | stories, social posts, podcasts, guides |
| Think | Consideration | problem aware, solution aware | aware of a need and researching options | informational to commercial | comparisons, how-to guides, calculators, case studies |
| Do | Decision | product aware, most aware | ready to choose and act | transactional | pricing page, demo or call, offer page, sample |
| Care | Retention | customers | using the product; deciding to stay, buy more or refer | navigational, support | onboarding, help, check-ins, referral kit |

## Search intent

| Intent | The searcher wants to… | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informational | learn or understand | "how to forecast cash flow for an agency" |
| Navigational | reach a specific site | "ledgerline login" |
| Commercial | compare options before buying | "best bookkeeping service for agencies" |
| Transactional | act now | "agency bookkeeping pricing", "buy coffee subscription" |

## Finding gaps

For each stage, check three things:
1. Is there an owned asset that answers this question?
2. Is the asset written for this stage's awareness level? A pricing page is wrong for a See-stage question.
3. Does evidence show buyers stalling here? Look at repeated questions on calls, lost-deal reasons and churn timing.

A stage with no asset, at a point where buyers stall, is the first gap to fill.
