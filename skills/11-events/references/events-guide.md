# Events guide

## Formats

| Format | Cost | Strength | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Booth or exhibitor stand | Fee, materials, travel, staff days | Volume of conversations if buyers walk the floor | Organiser attendance is a claim; many visitors are not buyers |
| Sponsorship | Fee | Logo reach | Rarely produces conversations by itself |
| Speaking slot | Time, often a free pass | Authority, a room that chose to listen | Application deadlines; talks must teach, not pitch |
| Attending | Ticket, time | Cheap way to test whether buyers are there | Needs pre-booked meetings to be worth the day |
| Hosted small event (breakfast, workshop) | Venue (or partner pays), time | Every attendee can fit the ICP | Attendance unknown until registrations |
| Local meetup | Free or low | Repeated contact with the same local buyers | Small rooms; works over months |

## Cost per conversation

Full cost = fees + travel + materials + staff days valued at what the owner says they cost.
Conversations needed = full cost / (value of a client × close rate from qualified conversation), using the business's own figures, labelled *calculated*, with the assumption shown. If any input is unknown, write the formula and ask.

## Before, during, after

- Before: a reason to visit (talk, resource that exists, short diagnostic); outreach to attendees who fit through channels the organiser allows; meetings booked.
- During: qualify each conversation against the ICP; capture notes the same day; book next steps on the spot.
- After: follow up within a few days, best fits first; send only what the attendee asked for or agreed to.

## Data

Attendee lists from organisers come with rules; badge scans are consent only for what the attendee was told. Verify privacy rules for the countries involved or mark *to confirm*.
