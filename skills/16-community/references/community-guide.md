# Community guide

## Formats

| Format | Good for | Host time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly call or peer session | Customers who asked to meet peers | Low |
| Customer advisory board | Input from best customers | Low, a few meetings a year |
| Ambassador program | Advocacy from committed customers | Medium |
| Event series | Local or industry groups | Medium |
| Online group (Slack, Discord, forum) | Daily peer help at scale | High; needs a host and rituals |

## Design

Purpose (one job), members (who's in and out), ritual (when people come), value exchange (what each side gets), rules (moderation, privacy).

## Measure

Active members per period, repeat attendance, member-to-member replies, and outcomes: retention and referrals of members versus non-members. Member count is vanity.
