# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Community-led growth

Growth that comes from members helping each other and bringing peers. Applied as one purpose, a ritual, an explicit value exchange, and engagement and outcomes measured.

## Ambassador programs

A small group of customers who represent and recommend the business, with recognition and access in return. Applied with clear expectations and no pay for opinions they don't hold.

## Customer advisory boards

A small group of customers who meet a few times a year to advise on product and direction. Applied when the business wants input and closer relationships with its best customers.
