# Visual direction

## Visual hierarchy

The eye reads in order of size, contrast, faces, movement and position. Decide the order on purpose:

1. **First:** the message carrier: a face showing the moment, the product in use, or a short line of text.
2. **Second:** the proof or the product.
3. **Third:** the brand cue (colour, wordmark, recurring device) and the call to action.

One focal point per frame. If everything is bold, nothing is.

## Short-form video structure

| Seconds (example) | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | Hook: seen, said and on-screen text carry one idea |
| Middle | Story: problem → turn → proof, one beat per shot |
| End | Offer: one CTA, the brand cue |

Lengths, aspect ratios and safe zones are platform specs: check the platform's own current documentation (URL and date) or write *to confirm*. Captions make it work with the sound off.

## Prompt anatomy for image or video generation

Subject · action · setting · composition and framing · lighting · style or medium · colour (brand codes from `brand.md` or inputs only) · aspect ratio · exclusions (no text, no logos, no real people's likeness, no brand marks).

Render text, numbers and logos separately as an overlay; generation models garble them and the overlay must be exact.

## Truth rules

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Show a labelled sample built from a dummy business | Show a "client" dashboard with invented numbers |
| Film real customers with consent | Generate a person and caption them as a customer |
| Use a real, typical before and after | Stage a contrast that exaggerates the result |
| License stock, music, fonts | Use another brand's logo, product or trade dress |
| Confirm AI-media disclosure rules for the platform and market | State the disclosure rule from memory |

## Brand cues

Distinctive assets work only when they are used the same way every time. Use what `brand.md` → Distinctive assets decides. If it is empty, use only what the inputs show in use, name inconsistencies (for example two brand colours in different places), and leave the decision to `05-distinctive-assets`.
