# Budget guide

## Affordability

Contribution margin per customer over the payback window ≥ target cost per new customer. Cash limits how fast you can wait for payback.

## Split (heuristic)

| Share | For |
| --- | --- |
| Most | Proven lines with a known cost per customer |
| Some | Promising lines being scaled through gates |
| A little | Experiments with a stop rule |

## Brand and activation

Binet and Field's published work suggests a balance between long-term brand building and short-term activation; the right balance for a small business is unknown without its own evidence. Label it.

## Reallocation

Write: if line X's cost per new customer exceeds guardrail G for N weeks, move M to line Y.
