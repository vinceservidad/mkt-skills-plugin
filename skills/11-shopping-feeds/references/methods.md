# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Shopping & product feeds

Structuring Google Merchant Center, primary product feeds, supplemental feeds and Shopping or Performance Max listing groups so high-margin, in-stock SKUs match commercial search intent cleanly. Applied as catalog hygiene first (excluding sold-out or broken SKUs), search-ordered supplemental titles without breaking storefront copy, custom-label segmentation by performance and margin, and Merchant Center checkout-parity verification.
