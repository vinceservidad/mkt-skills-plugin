# Attribution guide

## Views

| View | Bias |
| --- | --- |
| Platform-reported | Over-credits itself; view-through; its own windows |
| Last click (store or analytics) | Under-credits channels that start demand; untagged traffic lands in direct or none |
| Data-driven (in a tool) | Only sees touches it tracks |
| Self-reported survey | Recall bias; low response; good for podcasts and word of mouth |
| MMM | Needs long history and variation; slow; aggregate |
| Incrementality test | Strongest for one question; costs time and spend |

## Reconcile

Sum each view's credit for the period and compare with total orders from the system of record. Views that claim more than total orders are double counting or have a defect.

## MMM fit

Needs many weekly periods, multiple channels with real spend changes, and few confounders (cite a chosen framework's official docs with URL and access date if stating exact minimum-history rules; otherwise judge from the months present and mark *(assumption)*).

## Decisions

Routine: the decision view with its bias. Big moves (doubling, cutting a channel): an incrementality test.
