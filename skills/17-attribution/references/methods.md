# Methods this skill covers

Short definitions of the master-index methods this skill owns, and how it applies each one.

## Attribution

Assigning credit for conversions to channels and touches. Applied as several views (platform, last click, self-report) reconciled to the system of record, with one decision view and its bias stated.

## Marketing Mix Modeling

Statistical models that estimate each channel's contribution from spend and sales over time. Applied only when there is enough history and spend variation; otherwise its data needs are listed.
