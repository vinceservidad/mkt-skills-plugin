# Growth loops guide

## Types

| Loop | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Referral | Customer → shares → friend visits → subscribes → customer |
| Content | Content → search visit → subscriber → (feedback, reviews) → more content |
| Paid | Revenue → ad spend → new customer → contribution margin → revenue |
| Sales | Customer → case study → prospect → customer |

## Maths

Multiplier = new customers produced per customer per cycle. Paid loop closes when contribution margin within payback ≥ cost per new customer.

## Friction

Late deliveries, hard cancellation, poor first experience slow every loop. Remove before adding incentives.

## Incentives

Rewards can buy low-value or fraudulent referrals; judge on retained customers.
