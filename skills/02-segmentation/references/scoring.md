# Segment scoring

Score each segment from 1 to 3 on each criterion, using only the evidence you have. A score from inference carries `(assumption)`. Totals rank the segments; the reasons decide.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | Typical evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Size signal | few buyers or unknown | enough for the next 12 months | large or growing | market.md → Size, customer counts, lead volume |
| Pain and urgency | nice to have | recurring cost or frustration | acute, with a trigger event | interview triggers, churn reasons, ticket volume |
| Ability to pay | price is the main objection | pays with negotiation | pays today's price or more | closed deals, lost-on-price counts, current spend on alternatives |
| Reach | no known channel | reachable at a cost | gathers somewhere you can reach cheaply (referrals, communities) | lead sources, where quotes came from |
| Fit with today's offer | needs a different product | needs packaging changes | served well now | retention, reviews, churn reasons |
| Evidence strength | one anecdote | several sources agree | behaviour (purchases, churn) confirms it | number and type of sources |

## Bases, from most to least predictive (usually)

1. **Needs-based.** The job and outcome they want, for example "decide on hires weekly" or "books and tax only".
2. **Behavioural.** Trigger, usage frequency, current alternative, purchase history.
3. **Situational or firmographic.** Company size, stage, revenue model, industry. For consumers: life stage, household.
4. **Psychographic.** Values, identity, attitudes. Use only with quotes that show them.
5. **Demographic.** Age, gender, location. Use only when evidence shows they predict response.

## Common mistakes

- **Segments by demographics only,** such as "women 25–34", with no difference in need.
- **Too many segments to act on.** Each segment needs its own message, and often its own offer.
- **Choosing by size alone.** A large segment you cannot win, reach or serve profitably is the wrong target.
- **Treating a segment the business serves badly as a target.** Note it as "not now" and pass the gap to `07-offer` (packaging) or `04-product`.
