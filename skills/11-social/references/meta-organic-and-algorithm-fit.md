# Meta Organic Presence, Algorithm Distribution Fit and Organic-to-Paid Bridge

Distilled from the Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate study guide (January 2026) paired with distribution-fit diagnostics. Treat all ranking behaviors and optimal posting frequencies as platform claims to test in the account's own Insights rather than fixed rules.

## 1. Facebook Page, Instagram Profile and Business Suite readiness

Before investing weekly creative effort on Meta organic surfaces:
1. **Profile conversion readiness:** Verify business name parity, accurate category, clear what-you-do and who-it-for bio (`context/positioning.md`), current hours/contact channels, working tracked CTA link (`17-tracking-plan` UTM rule), and pinned proof or orientation posts for first-time profile visitors.
2. **Access and Business Suite hygiene:** Confirm Page and Instagram asset permissions inside Business Portfolio (never share personal logins), and connect Meta Business Suite inbox routing so comments, DMs, and support complaints flow to assigned owners with realistic response windows (`05-reputation` handles public disputes; `11-whatsapp` / `14-dm-selling` handle structured DM/chat flows).
3. **Never fabricate trust cues:** Never invent awards, service areas, hours, or testimonials to make a sparse profile appear active.

## 2. Communication jobs and algorithm distribution fit

Match every series in the content plan to a concrete buyer communication job grounded in customer evidence (`02-voc-mining`) and inspect whether the native format carries it:
- **Demonstrate product or mechanism:** Real-world use, tactile craft, or side-by-side workflow suited to short vertical Reels or step-by-step carousels.
- **Answer recurring sales/support questions:** Converts real objections (`08-objection-map`) into concise native answers.
- **Show process and team transparency:** Behind-the-scenes production, sourcing, or redaction-safe client walkthroughs that build credibility without hype.
- **Invite buyer conversation:** Polls, comparisons, or comment-prompting questions that surface VoC language directly in threads.

Evaluate distribution fit empirically per platform inside Insights: compare saves, shares, watch-through retention, profile clicks, and **attributed leads or orders** across formats (`scripts/sum_by.py`) over matched periods. Remember that high top-of-funnel views from non-ICP spectators do not build pipeline (`11-social` ranks formats by outcome first, reach second).

## 3. Organic-to-paid creative validation bridge

Organic social serves as a low-cost creative testing lab, with strict ownership and permission boundaries:
- **Shortlist candidates for paid:** When an organic post earns outsized ICP comments, saves, profile conversions, or outbound clicks relative to the account's own median, flag the angle and hook structure as a candidate concept for `09-ad-creative` and `11-paid-social`.
- **Replicate before crowning a pillar:** Treat one outlier post as a single observation until a second structured execution on the same angle confirms buyer interest.
- **Never boost blindly without rights or outcome goals:** Organic engagement optimization buys cheap likes rather than customers. Hand budget allocation and Sales/Leads conversion setup strictly to `11-paid-social`, and secure explicit permission before turning any customer comment, review, or tagged user clip into a paid ad (`20-compliance`).
