# Social: decision rules

## Platform choice

- Evidence first: lead sources in sales notes, journey touchpoints, where reviews and forum posts come from, the buyer's own mentions of where they read or watch.
- One or two platforms. A second platform is added when the first has a sustained cadence and a readable outcome.
- B2B owners and operators: the founder's personal profile often carries further than a company page (heuristic; check against the account's own analytics). Consumer products with a visual daily ritual: short video and image feeds (heuristic).

## Reading the account's own analytics

- Total by format and by topic with `scripts/sum_by.py`; never by eye.
- Rank on the outcome columns first (downloads, sign-ups, sourced calls or orders), then reach and reactions.
- Report counts. Ten posts in a quarter is a small sample: say the read is directional.
- Who engaged matters as much as how many. A post that drew freelancers and two-person studios when the ICP is 10–40 staff agencies is reach from the wrong audience.
- A single viral post is one observation. Before it becomes a series, post a second version and compare outcomes, not reach.
- Weeks with no posts and the effect of a gap are a hypothesis to test in the account, not a documented platform rule.

## Series and the give-to-ask mix

- A series is a recurring post type with a fixed shape: *the three numbers I check*, *a redacted month-end pack*, *a beginner's brew guide in 30 seconds*.
- Source each series from an evidenced pain, job, belief or question (cite IDs), or from a content-strategy pillar.
- Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook (Vaynerchuk): give value native to the platform several times for each ask. The ratio is a heuristic, not a rule; the point is that asks are rare and earned.
- Asks point only to free steps or offers that exist in `offer.md` (a checklist, a template, a sample, a subscription as it is). Anything else is a *gap* for `12-lead-magnet` or `07-offer`.

## Cadence and capacity

- Cadence = what the stated people and hours can sustain, minus the weeks the business is known to be busy.
- Replies count against the same hours. A post that draws 31 comments asking for a checklist creates reply work.
- When capacity is unknown, propose a minimal cadence marked *(assumption)* and ask.

## Community and replies

| Comment or message | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Asks for the free asset | Reply with the link or send it; this is the capture moment |
| Question the content can answer | Answer publicly in plain words |
| Support issue (an order, a billing problem, a skip or cancel) | Acknowledge publicly, move to support or DM, fix |
| Public complaint about the product or service | Acknowledge, take to support; if it is a reputation issue, `05-reputation` |
| Wrong-fit prospect (disqualified segment) | Be helpful; do not pitch the core offer |
| Abuse or spam | Follow the platform's tools; never argue |

Response times and who replies are operational commitments: propose them, the owner confirms. Never delete fair criticism, never promise terms `offer.md` doesn't hold.

## Platform claims

Distribution and ranking behaviour, format specs and lengths, feature availability, link handling and best times to post change and are documented only selectively. For each claim the plan relies on:

- **Documented:** the platform's own help, creator or transparency documentation, checked during this run, cited `(first-party: <URL>, checked YYYY-MM-DD)`.
- **Account-observed:** visible in the supplied analytics, with the date range.
- **Inferred** or **unknown:** say so, and write it as a test.

Third-party benchmarks (average engagement rates, best times to post) are not evidence about this account.

## Measurement

Per series: one outcome metric (sign-ups, downloads, sourced calls or orders) and its attribution method (UTM-tagged links, sign-up source, CRM "how did you hear about us"). If none exists, hand it to `17-tracking-plan`. Reach, impressions, reactions and follower growth are signals.
