# Epiphany Bridge (Brunson, Expert Secrets)

| Beat | What it holds | Belief it usually works on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backstory | who you were and what you believed, so the buyer recognises themselves | sets up the false belief |
| Desire | what you wanted | the buyer's dream outcome |
| Wall | the point where the old way failed | the vehicle belief starts to crack |
| Epiphany | the moment you saw a new way | the vehicle belief is rebuilt |
| Plan | what you did with the new idea | the mechanism |
| Conflict | what went wrong or was hard | internal belief ("I can't"): you struggled too |
| Achievement | the result | proof |
| Transformation | who you became, and who else it worked for | external belief ("it worked beyond me") |

## Three beliefs

- **Vehicle:** "This kind of solution won't work." Rebuilt by the epiphany.
- **Internal:** "I can't do it" or "I'll lose something." Rebuilt by the conflict and the plan.
- **External:** "Something outside me will stop it." Rebuilt by the transformation and other people's results.

## Questions to draw a true story out

Use these when there is no story material:

1. What were you doing before this business, and what did you believe about the problem then?
2. When did you first see the problem up close? Who was involved, what happened, and when?
3. What did you try first that didn't work?
4. What was the exact moment you saw it differently? What did you notice?
5. What did you do next? What was hard about it?
6. What changed, with a number if you have one, and for whom?
7. What do you refuse to do now, because of what you learned?
8. Who else did it work for, and what did they say, in their words?

## Honesty rules

- No invented events, dialogue, numbers, family members, feelings, motives or causal links (a refusal or value listed in the notes is not "because of" an event unless the notes say so).
- Compress and order real events, but don't merge different people into one character without saying so.
- Use a third party's story only with their permission.
