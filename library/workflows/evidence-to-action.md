# Evidence to finished marketing work

Primary knowledge type: methodology/process. This is a coordinated workflow for a requested marketing job, not proof of improved results or a scheduled agent. Use only the stages needed for the request. A simple copy revision does not require an account audit.

## Ownership and inputs

The Marketing Router selects one decision owner. Performance diagnosis belongs to `$performance-diagnostics`; creative to `$creative-strategy`; conversion to `$cro`; reporting to `$marketing-reporting`. Supporting owners resolve distinct dependencies. Require the goal, relevant product/audience truth, sources, dates and metric definitions when material, output requirements, constraints and authorization for any external change. Ask progressively rather than forcing an intake wizard.

## Workflow

1. **Establish evidence.** Record source identity, reporting basis, retrieval time, completeness and whether a statement is observed, calculated, user-reported, inferred or unknown. An upload, selected account, successful API call or user-written “verified” label does not establish business truth.
2. **Check measurement and economics.** Route collection/attribution questions to `$tracking-measurement`. Keep platform-attributed value separate from order revenue and qualified pipeline. Compare only aligned date, timezone, currency and metric bases. Withhold cross-source calculations for partial data. Net revenue already includes its stated refunds and discounts; never subtract them twice. Name the included costs and profit level before computing contribution.
3. **Diagnose and prioritize.** Present the evidence supporting each finding, plausible alternative explanations, the decision it changes, expected impact as an estimate where appropriate, effort, dependencies, owner and reversibility. Do not invent numeric opportunity or promote a heuristic into a fact.
4. **Produce finished work.** Return the requested brief, copy, implementation draft or stakeholder report, not only a methodology. Creative needs the angle, mechanic, concept, visual direction, supported proof, exact copy, CTA and production requirements. CRO needs actual journey evidence and specific proposed changes. Missing decision-changing facts remain visible.
5. **Define the test.** Name the primary business outcome, measurement validity requirements, comparator, variable, observation window, guardrails and decision rule. Preserve the account’s learning and useful coverage. Scaling remains under `$optimization-scaling`; no universal increase rule applies.
6. **Control execution and follow-up.** A proposal is a draft. Use only an actually available authorized runtime. The person reviews the exact account, entity, current state, proposed state, material risk and expiry. A revised or stale preview needs new approval. Verify provider state after a write. An uncertain outcome stops automatic retries. Recovery is another scoped decision and needs authorization. Report the result and retain scoped learning without claiming causality or general superiority.

## Runtime use

Local skills apply this workflow within their host's authorized tools. The hosted MCP `prepare_marketing_workflow` tool prepares skill ownership, stages and required artifacts; the host assistant completes them. Preparation is not a finished audit or generated creative. `reconcile_business_results` computes explicitly supplied comparable aggregates; it does not join orders or deduplicate channels.

## Output and QA

Return evidence/assumptions; supported findings and prioritized decisions; the requested finished deliverable; test/measurement plan; source and permission limits; exact implementation/verification state. Check that calculations match the source basis, the deliverable is complete for the requested scope, and a plan is never reported as applied or proven.
