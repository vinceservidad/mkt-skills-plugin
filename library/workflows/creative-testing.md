# Creative Testing Workflow

## Knowledge metadata

- Primary type: process
- Secondary type: experiment workflow / checklist
- Decision: what a creative test can validly teach, and what happens next with that learning
- Owner: `$creative-strategy`; `$tracking-measurement` owns test validity and the learning record; the channel skill owns platform fit and configuration
- Evidence status: hypothesis-generating until a valid test result exists; a result applies only to its tested scope
- Authorization: draft; launching, publishing, and spend changes require explicit approval

## Goal

Find creative angles that improve the primary business outcome, not only engagement.

## Process

1. **Ground the research.** Use verified customer language, awareness, and pain points. Take review evidence through the `$customer-research` handoff. Label gaps; do not invent voice-of-customer language.
2. **State the angle and hypothesis.** Name the audience situation, the angle, the expected mechanism, and what would disconfirm it.
3. **Control the variation.** Where practical, vary one major dimension per learning cell: angle, mechanic, opening, proof, format, offer framing, or CTA.
4. **Fix the decision rule before launch.** Name the primary metric tied to the business outcome, the success signal, business guardrails, the decision window including conversion lag, and the stop rule. Use the `$tracking-measurement` experiment brief.
5. **Validate platform fit and get approval.** The owning channel skill checks current platform fit. Nothing launches without explicit approval.
6. **Launch the approved test.**
7. **Check validity before direction.** `$tracking-measurement` confirms allocation, instrumentation, contamination, sample, and lag before anyone reads the result.
8. **Record a scoped learning.** Use the `$tracking-measurement` experiment learning record: validity class, observed result and uncertainty, guardrail status, observation kept separate from mechanism interpretation, tested scope (audience, platform, placement, format, offer, period), transfer status, and contradictions. Keep nulls and losses.
9. **Iterate or hand off.** Iterate from a winning cell by holding the validated variable fixed and changing one further dimension (`$creative-strategy` Iterating from a Winner reference). A win does not authorize more spend. Scaling spend or exposure behind a winning cell goes to `$optimization-scaling`, including its creative-capacity gate.

## Evaluation

Check:

- Is the audience clear?
- Is the insight supported by traceable evidence?
- Is the offer strong, and is the offer itself owned by `$offer-strategy` if it changes?
- Is the variable controlled?
- Was validity assessed before the result was read?
- Is the learning stated within its tested scope, without claiming a universal best practice?
- Does the result support a decision, and who owns that decision?
