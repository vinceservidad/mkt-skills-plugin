# Creative Brief

## Knowledge metadata

- Primary type: template
- Secondary type: planning record / test design
- Decision: whether a creative concept is supportable, testable, and ready for approval
- Owner: `$creative-strategy`; the channel skill owns platform fit, and `$tracking-measurement` owns test validity and the learning record
- Evidence status: reusable structure; label each research input and claim `observed`, `inferred`, `assumed`, or `unknown`
- Authorization: draft until approved; producing, publishing, and spend require explicit approval

Reusable structure for turning customer research into testable creative ideas.

## Purpose

Define what creative should communicate, how the idea should make the message land, why it is supportable, and how success will be measured.
